Taxon explorer

Taxon explorer · VANISH / BloSSUM · Replication of published findings · Taxon map · PCA of community profiles · Profile coverage and quality

Every genus and species the community profiles detect — 8,392 genera and 24,324 species from the 379,776 samples that passed the profile quality checks, of the catalog's 813,356 — placed on the GTDB R232 reference tree, with the branch lengths kept. Search a name, or open the taxonomy list or the tree and click a genus or species: its charts, map and summary then fill the page.

    Names used in papers work too: an older name such as Bacteroides vulgatus finds the current Phocaeicola vulgatus.

    How this is computed

    Community profiles come from Sandpiper (SingleM, GTDB R232). A sample counts here when it is a specimen (not a control), has a profile, and carries none of these QC flags: low depth, non-metagenome organism label, synthetic, RNA library, ecological prediction, no genus assigned, low complexity. That leaves 379,776 samples in 2,905 projects. Profile coverage and quality applies the same seven flags to every profiled sample, controls and mock communities included, so its count is a little higher; the other Insights pages use different subsets again. Computed on data package 1.34.2 (release R2026.43).

    Prevalence is the share of samples in a group with a relative abundance above zero. Mean relative abundance averages over every sample in the group, counting undetected samples as zero. The body site chart is over all QC-clean samples; age group, country and lifestyle are computed within the samples of the taxon's leading body site, which is named above each of those charts. A group is drawn only with at least 30 samples in at least 2 projects; smaller groups are listed as “too few samples”. 3,125 of the 32,716 taxa have no body site that passes the threshold and so have no age, country or lifestyle chart. This page shows no result by health condition.

    The tree is the GTDB R232 bacterial and archaeal reference tree pruned to these taxa, 64,519 nodes and 28,814 tips, with every branch length preserved. Bacteria and Archaea are drawn as two trees, each with its own scale bar: the reference trees have no common root, so a distance read across the break would be meaningless. A genus with species in the catalog sits on the common ancestor of those species; 4,490 genera with no species row sit on the tip of one representative genome.

    Limits

    The catalog is a convenience sample of public data, not a survey. Body site, age, country and lifestyle are curated from the projects’ own records and are missing for many samples. Relative abundances depend on the reference database, so a taxon with few reference genomes reads as less abundant than it is. Lifestyle groups are small and come from a handful of projects, and countries differ in which projects were run there, so a difference between groups is not by itself a difference between populations. 428 taxa carry a short summary written from the published literature by an AI model; those statements are published findings about the taxon, not results computed here.
