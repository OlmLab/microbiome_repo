Taxon map — where taxa are carried

Taxon explorer · Condition explorer · VANISH / BloSSUM · Replication of published findings · Taxon map · PCA of community profiles · Profile coverage and quality

Pick a taxon and the map recolours every country by how often it is detected in that country's metagenomes. This view uses 367,485 of the catalog's 813,356 samples: every profiled sample that states a body site and passes the profile quality flags, at all body sites; the other Insights pages use different subsets, and Profile coverage and quality counts every profiled sample.

About this page

Data: community profiles from Sandpiper 2.0.1 (GTDB R232, Zenodo 21287531), rebuilt in every release, for 367,485 catalog samples in 2,754 projects and 114 countries; the map downloads 26.2 MB the first time, and one more file each time you change rank. Computed on data package 1.35.0 (release R2026.44).

Species: the 500 most prevalent species (detection ≥ 0.1 %).
    Countries with fewer than 30 samples are grey. Prevalence = the share of a country's samples carrying the taxon at ≥ 0.01 % (genus and above) or ≥ 0.1 % (species). The Taxon explorer counts any detection, so its prevalences are higher.
    Project dossier
    Click a country to list its projects, then a project for its dossier (n samples, prevalence, mean abundance, age groups, link to the project page).
    About this view — read before interpreting a map
    • Project is the master confounder. Most projects sample one country and one age group with one protocol; a country's colour is the mixture of its few projects (click a country to see them). Compare countries only when the project list looks comparable, and prefer the per-project dossiers.
    • Repeated measures. Longitudinal cohorts contribute many samples per person; n samples overstates the number of people.
    • Depth. Detection at the threshold above depends on sequencing depth; shallow projects under-detect rare taxa.
    • Which samples are mapped. Sandpiper profiles Illumina runs ≥ 100 Mbp public before its snapshot. A sample is on the map when its record states a body site — any site — and its profile carries none of six quality flags: non-metagenome organism label, synthetic, RNA library, ecological prediction, no genus assigned, and low depth (a root coverage below 2 — root coverage is how many times over the marker genes were sequenced, so 2 is roughly two genome equivalents). Low-complexity profiles are kept here; the Taxon explorer and Profile coverage and quality leave them out.
    • Taxonomy. Names follow GTDB R232, so Prevotella copri is Prevotella, Bacteroides vulgatus is Phocaeicola vulgatus, and suffixed genera (Blautia_A) are GTDB placeholders.
    File layout and map notes
    • Hong Kong and Singapore are drawn as dots (the 110 m base map merges them into larger polygons). Country is missing for 5.3 % of samples.
    • Boundaries. Country shapes are from Natural Earth (1:110 m) and are shown only to display sample counts; they imply no position on the status of any territory. Crimea is drawn as part of Ukraine (UN General Assembly resolution 68/262). A sample is placed by the country its record states.
    • Payload. data/atlas/taxa.json (index), countries.json, studies.json, matrix_<rank>.bin — format documented in taxa.json → meta.format; HDI values in country_hdi.csv (UNDP Human Development Report 2025, Statistical Annex Table 1 (HDI 2023); band: high >= 0.80, middle 0.70-0.79, low < 0.70; Taiwan (statistics office, 2023), Puerto Rico (published calculation for 2015) and French Guiana (Global Data Lab subnational HDI, 2023) are not UNDP values; TC and IO have no value and no band; 111 of the 114 countries on this map have a value).
