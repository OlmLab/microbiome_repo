A seeded random 330,000 of the 404,318 catalog samples whose Sandpiper community profile is deep enough to score — root coverage, how many times over the marker genes were sequenced, of at least 2 — plotted on the first five principal components of the centred log-ratio transformed genus table. That is one subset of the catalog's 813,356 samples; the other Insights pages use different ones, and every score is in the download.

About this page

330,000 of the 813,356 catalog samples across 3,013 projects. To keep the page light it draws a seeded random 330,000 of the 404,318 scored samples; every score is in catalog_sandpiper_pca_scores.parquet. Colour by curated metadata, hover a point for its sample and project, type a project accession to draw its samples on top, or drag a box to count a selection. Everything runs in your browser; the data files total 13.7 MB. Computed on data package 1.35.0 (release R2026.44).

Limits

Project is the master confounder: most projects sample one country, one age group and one protocol with one laboratory method, so a cluster of points is often a project. Longitudinal cohorts contribute many samples per person, so the number of points overstates the number of people. Nearby points have similar genus compositions; the distance between them is not a calibrated ecological measure.

Method

Sandpiper 2.0.0 GTDB R232 profiles of every profiled run of the catalog (catalog_sandpiper_sample_genus.parquet), summed over a sample's runs and normalised by the sample's root coverage. Samples with root coverage ≥ 2 genome equivalents enter the matrix; genera present at ≥ 0.1 % relative abundance in at least 1 % of these samples are kept (386 genera), re-closed to 1, zeros replaced by a multiplicative pseudocount of 1e-5, log-transformed and centred per sample (CLR), then columns centred and decomposed by randomised SVD (5 components). Only the retained genera (not the unassigned remainder) enter the composition. Points are scores; loadings above are genus weights per component. Nearby points have similar genus compositions; distance is not a calibrated ecological metric — see the Sandpiper profile report.

File layout

The files this page reads (they are in the downloads): pca_points.bin (6,600,000 bytes) = little-endian Float32[n_points × n_pcs], row-major, point i component j at i·n_pcs+j; pca_codes.bin (2,640,000 bytes) = one categorical column per field in the order study_accession, age_category, country, health_condition, body_site_class, top_genus, lifestyle, each Uint16 (project) or Uint8 (others) × n_points at the byte offset given in pca_meta.json → fields[k].offset; codes index fields[k].labels, whose last two entries are always other and (no value); pca_meta.json (5,135,794 bytes) carries the sample keys in point order (newline-joined), explained variance, loadings and project titles. Total 14,375,794 bytes.
