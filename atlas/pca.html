About this page

330,000 of the 806,054 catalog samples across 3,004 studies. To keep the page light it draws a seeded random 330,000 of the 404,897 scored samples; every score is in catalog_sandpiper_pca_scores.parquet. Colour by curated metadata, hover a point for its sample and study, type a study accession to draw its samples on top, or drag a box to count a selection. Everything runs in your browser; the data files total 13.7 MB.

Method

Sandpiper 2.0.0 GTDB R232 profiles of every profiled run of the catalog (catalog_sandpiper_sample_genus.parquet), summed over a sample's runs and normalised by the sample's root coverage. Samples with root coverage ≥ 2 genome equivalents enter the matrix; genera present at ≥ 0.1 % relative abundance in at least 1 % of these samples are kept (386 genera), re-closed to 1, zeros replaced by a multiplicative pseudocount of 1e-5, log-transformed and centred per sample (CLR), then columns centred and decomposed by randomised SVD (5 components). Only the retained genera (not the unassigned remainder) enter the composition. Points are scores; loadings above are genus weights per component. Nearby points have similar genus compositions; distance is not a calibrated ecological metric — see Methods.

Data layout under data/atlas/: pca_points.bin (6,600,000 bytes) = little-endian Float32[n_points × n_pcs], row-major, point i component j at i·n_pcs+j; pca_codes.bin (2,640,000 bytes) = one categorical column per field in the order study_accession, age_category, country, health_condition, body_site_class, top_genus, lifestyle, each Uint16 (study) or Uint8 (others) × n_points at the byte offset given in pca_meta.json → fields[k].offset; codes index fields[k].labels, whose last two entries are always other and (no value); pca_meta.json (5,134,801 bytes) carries the sample keys in point order (newline-joined), explained variance, loadings and study titles. Total 14,374,801 bytes.
