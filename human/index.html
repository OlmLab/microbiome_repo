Every human body site

This scope holds every human-associated shotgun metagenome in the registry — any body site, DNA and RNA kept as separate data sets — together with the sequenced mock communities used as benchmarks. The gut projects are the curated gut catalog; the rest were classified and read in this release.

DNA and RNA

DNA · 4,482 projects RNA · 292 projects DNA and RNA · 173 projects sources disagree · 1 project

Nucleic acid belongs to a data set, not to a specimen: one stool sample can carry a DNA library and an RNA library, and they are recorded separately. The value comes from the archive's library source, with the paper's methods as the tie-breaker; 1 project where the two disagree are shown in neither the DNA nor the RNA view until the disagreement is resolved. Runs: 935,933 DNA, 31,206 RNA.
