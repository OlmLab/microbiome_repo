Registry: every screened shotgun-metagenome study

Every screened shotgun-metagenome project in ENA, NCBI SRA and DDBJ (64,369), classified for human host, body site, life stage and assay; ✓ marks the projects in the catalog.

About this page

Is the registry all human? No. The registry holds every shotgun-metagenome study reached by the enumeration — INSDC BioProjects deposited in ENA, NCBI SRA or DDBJ and enumerated through ENA's mirror — and classifies each for human host (registry_studies.host_human): 6,649 host human = yes, 244 mixed (human and non-human material), 13,697 unknown and 43,779 no, out of 64,369 studies in release R2026.19. The 6,893 human studies (yes + mixed) are the headline number on this site; the explorer below shows them by default and lets you include the unknown and non-human rows.

Registry explorer

Filters the 64,369 registry studies in your browser — by default the 6,893 human studies (host human = yes or mixed); tick unknown / no to widen. Runs using DuckDB-WASM over data/registry_studies.parquet (9.0 MB); nothing is sent to a server. Click a row for every column, the evidence rows behind host / body site / life stage. The URL updates with your filters so you can share a view. Registry-only studies have no page on this site (they link to ENA); catalog studies link to their study page. Opening registry/index.html?study=PRJ… filters to that study and opens its record.

✓ = in the curated catalog (has a project page with per-sample metadata). Click any row to see why a study is — or is not — in the catalog.

What a registry row is

Each study carries archive-only metadata (route R1): host, body site(s) anchored to UBERON, life stage(s), assay, access, and the classification stage that produced them, each committed value with an evidence row (labelled source, ≤ 12-word quote). Registry rows are classifications, not inclusions: a study can carry several body sites and life stages; rows at stage pending have not been classified yet. The curated tier goes deeper — per-sample fields recovered from supplementary tables and papers (routes R2–R4) — and is built scope by scope; the catalog (human gut, all ages: 2,865 studies with study pages) is the first curated scope. See Methods › Registry classification for the cascade, the vocabularies and what the confidence means.
