Child / adolescent gut (3–18 y) scope

Human gut/stool shotgun metagenomes from children and adolescents.

Scope code: gut_child (used in the registry table and the API).

A study is in this scope if the archive record puts it in the gut or stool and names a child or adolescent. Memberships derive deterministically from host, body sites, life stages and assay; a study can belong to several scopes. Studies of this scope that are in the catalog link to a project page with per-sample metadata; the rest are registry-only — the table below marks which is which.
