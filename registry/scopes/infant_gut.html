Infant gut (0–3 y) scope

Human gut/stool shotgun metagenomes from people aged 0–1,100 days.

Scope code: infant_gut (used in the registry table and the API).

A study is in this scope if it was curated in the infant gut set — the one scope whose membership comes from the curation rather than from the archive record. Memberships derive deterministically from host, body sites, life stages and assay; a study can belong to several scopes. Studies of this scope that are in the catalog link to a project page with per-sample metadata; the rest are registry-only — the table below marks which is which.
