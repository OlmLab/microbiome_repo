All human shotgun metagenomes (registry)

Every screened study with at least one human-host shotgun metagenome run — the human studies minus those whose deposits are amplicon, isolate or assembly-only; metadata from the archive record alone, plus Sandpiper profiles, papers and authors.

Scope code: human_all (used in the registry table and the API).

A study is in this scope if the archive record names a human or mixed host and shotgun DNA sequencing, or the study is in one of the curated scopes. Memberships derive deterministically from host, body sites, life stages and assay; a study can belong to several scopes.
