Respiratory tract respiratory curated scope

Nasal / nasopharyngeal and lower-respiratory shotgun metagenomes.

Membership rule (config/scope.yaml): nasal_nasopharyngeal in body_sites OR respiratory_lower in body_sites. Memberships derive deterministically from host, body sites, life stages and assay; a study can belong to several scopes. This scope is curated: every included study has a study page with per-sample metadata.
