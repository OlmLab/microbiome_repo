Oral cavity scope

Saliva, plaque, tongue and other oral-cavity shotgun metagenomes (all ages).

Scope code: oral (used in the registry table and the API).

A study is in this scope if the archive record puts it in the oral cavity. Memberships derive deterministically from host, body sites, life stages and assay; a study can belong to several scopes. Studies of this scope that are in the catalog link to a project page with per-sample metadata; the rest are registry-only — the table below marks which is which.
