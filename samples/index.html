Sample sheet (582,739 samples)

One row per curated sample of the 2,886 catalog studies. Filters run in your browser with DuckDB-WASM over data/gut_sample_metadata_wide.parquet (17.0 MB); nothing is sent to a server. Click a row for every field with its route and confidence; the verbatim evidence quotes are in gut_sample_determinations.parquet (28.8 MB, Downloads) and per study on the study pages.

Built with an LLM-assisted pipeline; treat individual values as evidence-linked claims, not ground truth. Found an error?
