About this page

Filters run in your browser with DuckDB-WASM over data/catalog_sample_metadata_wide.parquet (29.7 MB); nothing is sent to a server. Click a row for every field with its source and confidence; the verbatim evidence quotes are on each sample page, and per project on the project pages.

Each value carries a badge saying where it came from: archive record · supplementary table · paper text · abstract. Click a row for every field; the sample accession links to its BioSample record (NCBI / ENA / DDBJ). Gbp = billions of bases sequenced for this sample. The CSV export carries every column of the table plus the project title.
