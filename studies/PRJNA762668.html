Archive description

Profiling of the early bacterial microbiota composition from stool samples of Tunisian newborns using shotgun metagenomic sequencing. Samples were collected from 5 neonates born by elective cesareans and 5 others vaginally delivered at Day0, Day15, and Day30.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of neonate and infant / toddler by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic. Centre Institute Pasteur of Tunis, first public 2022-04-08.
