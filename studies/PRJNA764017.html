Archive description

The data includes metagenomics of throat swabs from fever patients, anal swabs from fever patients, and throat swab sampling kits.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human oral cavity / other human site / multiple sites / nasal / nasopharyngeal / gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.65. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · population flags: disease_cohort.
