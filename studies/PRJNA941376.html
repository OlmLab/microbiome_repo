Archive description

In this research, a total of 61 samples were selected from Zhangjiang, Sanlin and Beicai communities respectively, and divided into lean NAFLD and non-lean NAFLD groups using a case-control study, and correlation analysis was performed by intestinal macrogenomics and clinical biochemical indicators to identify key characteristics of lean NAFLD population.
