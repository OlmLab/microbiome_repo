Sampling design and relatedness

longitudinalnan

39 of 39 people sampled more than once (114 samples; median 3, up to 4 per person) · follow-up median 3.6 y (max 4.7 y) · median interval between samples 12 mo (time from age at collection). Schedule stated by the source: “Up to 4 stool timepoints/patient: T1/T2 before ELX/TEZ/IVA (median ~33.6 and ~0.64 months before), T3/T4 at ~6 and ~12 months after initiation” (time origin: intervention_start).

Each row is one person (36 shown of 39); dots are samples at days since that person's first sample.
