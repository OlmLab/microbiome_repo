Archive description

The BRAINRISE is a unified proposal from the University of Sao Paulo to integrate research teams from different Departments across different disciplines. Researchers are national and international experts in the fields of early microbiome, childhood development, childhood mental disorders, language development, neurophysiology, neuropsychopharmacology, neurogenetics, biochemistry, neuromathematics, and network science/statistics
