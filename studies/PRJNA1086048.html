Details
Archive description

This project collected and profiled paired shotgun metagenomes from subjects with inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) and non-IBD controls. We uncovered fecal bacterial markers for IBD and validated their accuracy in IBD diagnosis.

How this project entered the registry

Classified as human and non-human (mixed) gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.70. Assay shotgun_dna.
