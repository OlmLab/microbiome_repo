Archive description

This project aims to investigate alterations in the gut microbiota and fecal metabolome associated with low bone mass in human fracture patients. Using an integrated multi-omics approach, we conducted shotgun metagenomic sequencing and untargeted metabolomics analysis on fecal samples collected from hospitalized patients stratified by bone mineral density (BMD). The study identified distinct microbial and metabolic signatures that differentiate patients with osteopenia and osteoporosis from those with normal bone mass. Notably, a four-species microbial signature, including the paradoxically enriched Lachnospira eligens, demonstrated high diagnostic accuracy for low bone mass status. This project provides novel insights into the gut-bone axis and proposes potential non-invasive biomarkers for skeletal health monitoring. The generated data contribute to advancing our understanding of the microbiome's role in bone metabolism, especially in the context of fracture recovery and age-related bone loss.
