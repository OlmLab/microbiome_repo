Archive description

Traditional human communities have shown more microbiome similarities with nonhuman primates in comparison to industrialized populations. In this study, we have profiled the fecal metagenomes of BaAka hunter-gatherers and Bantu agriculturalists from Central Africa and compared with the sympatric gorillas across seasons of variable dietary intake to understand this functional convergence.
