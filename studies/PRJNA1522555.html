Details
Archive description

This project uses oral saliva as a medium and employs metagenomic techniques to detect changes in the microbial community before and after neoadjuvant immunochemotherapy. The aim is to characterize the composition and relative abundance of the oral microbial community in oral cancer patients before and after neoadjuvant immunochemotherapy, compare the differences in the microbial community before and after treatment, and provide clinical evidence to inform future clinical treatments and decision-making.
