Details
Archive description

For viral metagenomic analysis, cerebrospinal fluid specimens were collected from patients with encephalitis of unknown etiology in China. The CSF samples were pooled into one specimen pool and thoroughly mixed, resuspended in 1 mL of DPBS, and subjected to three rapid freeze-thaw cycles on dry ice.
