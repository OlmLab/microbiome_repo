Archive description

A longitudinal study for strain-level characterization of Klebsiella pneumoniae from enriched stool culture samples for a cohort of healthy and hospitalized subjects residing in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Stool samples were collected on Day 0, 7 and 30 and cultured on Simmons Citrate Inositol agar. DNA extracted from plate-swipe was used for sequencing.
