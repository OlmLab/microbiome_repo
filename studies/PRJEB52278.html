Details
Archive description

50 human fluid samples, including 20 bronchoalveolar lavage fluid (BALF) samples, 21 urine samples and 9 blood samples, were collected in this study. The viral nucleic acid sequences were detected by mNGS with mode of single-end 50bp sequencing for the MGISEQ-200 platform.
