Archive description

The project conducts metagenomic sequencing of gut microbiota based on the data of Healthy Lifestyle Promotion Cohort, which recruited general adults in China. This project plans to jointly analyze these gut microbiome data and population metadata, with the main aim of understanding the potential mechanisms underlying multiple chronic and mental diseases, thereby providing a basis for disease prevention and control.
