Archive description

This project comprises shotgun metagenomic sequencing of stool samples from 89 patients with Crohn's disease receiving biologic therapies. The dataset was generated to characterize the gut microbiome and to investigate treatment-associated microbial signatures and their association with composite clinical and biochemical remission.
