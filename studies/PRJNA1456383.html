Archive description

This dataset includes fecal metagenomic sequencing from sepsis patients with or without sepsis-induced acute kidney injury, aiming to further identify microbial markers specifically associated with sepsis-induced kidney injury.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
