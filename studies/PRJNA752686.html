Details
Archive description

This cross-sectional study aimed to evaluate the metagenomic potential of the gut microbiome of preterm infants consuming human milk or infant formula during hospitalization in the neonatal intensive care unit. A total of 42 stool samples were analyzed using shotgun genomic DNA sequencing.
