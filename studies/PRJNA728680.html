Archive description

Randomized clinical trial of fecal microbiota transplant (FMT) for eradication of intestinal multi-drug resistant organism (MDRO) colonization after infection.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and elderly by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Emory University, first public 2022-07-06. Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: randomized_controlled_trial. FMT vs delayed FMT for MDRO decolonization in transplant recipients Trial population: transplant_or_immunocompromised.

Evidence (abstract:37910603, route R4, confidence 0.55): “randomized 1:1 to FMT or an observation period”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
