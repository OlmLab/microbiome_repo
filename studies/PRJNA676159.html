Archive description

Ten stool samples from self-reported healthy individuals were characterized with both 16S V4 rRNA gene sequencing (2x250 bp, MiSeq) and shotgun metagenomic sequencing (2x150 bp, Illumina NextSeq) to facilitate the evaluation of sequencing depth on the recovery of taxonomic and functional information.
