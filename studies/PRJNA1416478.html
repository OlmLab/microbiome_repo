Archive description

Metagenomic sequencing was performed on fecal samples in this study, with the aim of disclosing the significant association between the personalized effect of Lacticaseibacillus paracasei 207-27 on functional constipation and distinct microbiome signatures.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
