Archive description

Stool samples from participants in the CARMA study in all which participants were treated with vancomycin for seven days, and subsequently randomised to 2'-FL prebiotic or placebo intervention groups.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: antibiotic_trial. Centre Maastricht University Medical Center+;MUMC+, first public 2026-09-18. Curated source gut_all_v1.

Interventions

This study administered: Antibiotic given as the study intervention (incl. eradication therapy Prebiotic / dietary fibre supplement Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Vancomycin 7 days, then randomised to 2'-FL prebiotic vs placebo Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (bioproject.description, route R4, confidence 0.5): “treated with vancomycin for seven days, and subsequently randomised”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
