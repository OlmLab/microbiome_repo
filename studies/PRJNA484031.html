Archive description

62 fecal samples of low-set rectal cancer patients with FOLFOX treatment. Based on the clinical treatment result, the samples were clustered to R and NR. Analysis the gut microbiota, to illustrate the inter relationship between microbes and clinical results.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and elderly by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.68. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Institute of Microbiology, CAS, first public 2019-09-02.
