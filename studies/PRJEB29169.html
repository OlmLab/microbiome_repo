Details
Archive description

Unstimulated saliva was collected from 10 individuals 9 times over the course of the day. Salivary flow rate, microbial load quantification, 16S rRNA gene amplicon sequencing, shotgun metagenomics, and untargeted LC/MS-MS was performed on each sample.
