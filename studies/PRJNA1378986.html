Archive description

The gut microbiome plays a central role in digestion, early immune development, and the production of key metabolites that influence infant health. As part of the VITALITY Project, infant stool samples were collected longitudinally across the first year of life to capture gut microbiome development. This dataset contains shotgun metagenomic sequencing data that supports analyses aimed at identifying microbial features associated with infant atopic diseases, including atopic dermatitis, food sensitization, and food allergy.
