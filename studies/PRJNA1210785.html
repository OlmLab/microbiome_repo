Archive description

Background pSS is a chronic systemic inflammatory autoimmune disease and has been linked to the gut microbiota in animal models and adults. However, the characteristics of the gut microbiota and metabolites and the relationship to pSS patients from Northwest China remain unclear.

How this study entered the registry
