Archive description

In a Phase I clinical trial, 36 healthy subjects received a CRISPR-armed bacteriophage cocktail targeting E. coli, administered twice daily for seven days at three different dose-levels (or placebo). Deep metagenomic sequencing of stool samples was performed at multiple time points, before, during, and after the treatment, to assess the potential impact on the microbiome.</p><p>Human genetic material has been eliminated.
