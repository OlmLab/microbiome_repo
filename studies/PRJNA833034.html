Archive description

This study explored the alterations of gut microbiome in a cohort of adult patients with craniopharyngioma using the high-throughput sequencing of 16S rRNA gene sequencing and metagenomics approaches.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.75.
