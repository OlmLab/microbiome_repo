Details
Archive description

Sequencing data for gut metagenomes of 109 Singaporean subjects from the SPMP study. The project includes read data for Illumina, ONT and Hi-C sequencing. Metagenome assembled genomes (MAG) can be found in the "Related ENA Records" section, under "Analysis".

How this project entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Assay shotgun_dna.
