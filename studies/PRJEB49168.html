Archive description

Sequencing data for gut metagenomes of 109 Singaporean subjects from the SPMP study. The project includes read data for Illumina, ONT and Hi-C sequencing. Metagenome assembled genomes (MAG) can be found in the "Related ENA Records" section, under "Analysis".

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic. Centre GENOME INSTITUTE OF SINGAPORE, first public 2021-12-10. Curated source gut_all_v1.
