Archive description

The goal of the LOGIC study is to examine associations between health behaviors and variations in the gut microbiome among gastrointestinal cancer survivors.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.60. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: gastrointestinal cancer survivors, health behaviors and microbiome · population flags: disease_cohort.
