Details
Archive description

This project includes shotgun metagenomic sequencing data from human skin samples collected from patients with scabies before and after scabicidal treatment. Skin samples were obtained from dry and moist anatomical sites to characterize scabies-associated skin microbiome dysbiosis and treatment-associated microbial recovery. The study analyzed taxonomic composition, microbial burden, alpha and beta diversity, and functional pathway potential across pre-treatment dry-site, pre-treatment moist-site, and post-treatment moist-site samples.
