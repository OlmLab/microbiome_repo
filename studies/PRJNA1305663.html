Archive description

The purpose of this study is to investigate the changes in gut microbiota and corresponding metabolites in patients with biliary atresia before and after KPE surgery.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by opus_adjudicated with confidence 0.75. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-opus-5-5 · health context: Biliary atresia patients before/after Kasai portoenterostomy, and controls · population flags: disease_cohort.
