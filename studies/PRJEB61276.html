Archive description

16S rDNA profile and shotgun metagenomics of stool of COVID-19 patients supplemented with Nicotinamide (NAM) and a Placebo during 4 weeks of intervention.

This study administered: Non-microbial dietary supplement (vitamins Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Nicotinamide 1000mg vs placebo, 4 weeks, in COVID-19 outpatients Trial population: other_infection.

Evidence (abstract:40355744, route R4, confidence 0.55): “double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trial”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
