Archive description

Additional sequencing of stool metagenomes from the USDA's Nutritional Phenotyping Study (ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT02367287). Previous sequencing of 290 individuals are attached to the BioProject PRJNA795985.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.80. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · population flags: healthy_volunteers. Centre University of California, Davis, first public 2024-09-29. Curated source gut_all_v1.
