Archive description

We analyzed the dynamics of serum and fecal metabolites in a prospective cohort of quiescent CD patients over 2 years or until the occurrence of a clinical flare, aiming to identify whether changes precede and predict relapse. We further defined complex interactions between serum and fecal metabolites and the gut microbiome, and signals linked with CD. Here are 138 metagenome gut microbiome samples from this study.
