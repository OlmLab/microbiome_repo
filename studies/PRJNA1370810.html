Archive description

Vitamin B2 (riboflavin) is a key redox cofactor that may modulate gut microbial ecology, yet conventional supplements are absorbed proximally and have limited colonic exposure. We evaluated whether colon-targeted riboflavin alters microbiome composition, function and network structure as well as host biomarkers in healthy older adults.
