Details
Archive description

Anellovirus genomic survey of the National Heart, Lung &amp Blood Institute's (NHLBI) longitudinal Transfusion-transmitted Viruses Study (TTVS). 128 samples from the study cohort were sequenced to isolate Anellovirus content with the aim of exploring transmission, persistence and dynamics across the longitudinal data set.
