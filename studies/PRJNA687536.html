Details
Archive description

Fecal samples were collected from 22 healthy and 24 Crohn's disease patients for metagenomic shotgun sequencing. Inclusion criteria for Crohn's disease included active endoscopic ileal disease consistent with a clinical diagnosis of ileal or ileo-colonic Crohn's disease and a Harvery-Bradshaw score of >4 indicative of active disease. Healthy controls were age-matched controls without evidence of intestinal inflammation or a history of CD.
