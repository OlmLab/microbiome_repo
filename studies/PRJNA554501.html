Interventions

This study administered: Probiotic (live microorganisms; single strain or mixture) Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Probiotic vs placebo during 30-day sea voyage in sailors Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (abstract:32079472, route R4, confidence 0.55): “a placebo group ( n = 42) and a probiotic group”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
