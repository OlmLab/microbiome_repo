Archive description

Fecal samples from 124 adolescents aged 12-16 years in China were collected for 16S rRNA sequencing, metagenomic sequencing, and liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry. Chronic stress was measured by the Adolescent Life Events Scale and the Study Stress Scale.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adolescent by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.75. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: Chronic stress in adolescents · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre xi'an jiaotong university, first public 2024-03-06. Curated source gut_all_v1.
