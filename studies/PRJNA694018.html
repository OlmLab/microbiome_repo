Archive description

Fecal samples were collected during 38th Indian Scientific Expedition to Antarctica from healthy volunteers, to study the gut microbial changes during ship voyage towards Antarctica. The samples were collected at different time points. Samples were analyzed for shotgun sequencing metagenome.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.57. Interventions

This study administered: Probiotic (live microorganisms; single strain or mixture) Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Probiotic vs placebo during Antarctic ship voyage Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (abstract:34552104, route R4, confidence 0.55): “effect of probiotic intervention on gut microbiota”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
