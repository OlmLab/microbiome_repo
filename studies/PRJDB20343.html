Oral (saliva) and stool samples were collected from colon cancer patients undergoing surgery before, and 1, 7 and 28 days after, administration of antibiotic prophylaxis, to assess effects on oral and gut microbiomes/resistomes. machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: Before antibiotic prophylaxis · 1 day after antibiotic prophylaxis · 7 days after antibiotic prophylaxis · 28 days after antibiotic prophylaxis

In the data: 8 people in 2 arms (Antibiotic given as the study intervention 8; Control / pre-intervention baseline 8); 8 sampled more than once (median 8, up to 8 samples per person), followed for a median of 28 days; 2 body sites (oral cavity, gut / stool); 8 people sampled at more than one site.

Details
Archive description

This project includes the metagenome data of oral and stool samples which were collected from colon cancer patients to assess the effects of antibiotic prophylaxis during colon cancer surgery on the oral and gut microbiomes and resistomes of patients.
