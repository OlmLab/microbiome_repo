Archive description

This study aimed at confirming the impact of the resistant dextrin NUTRIOSE® (RD), a soluble fiber with demonstrated beneficial health effects, on the gut microbiota of healthy individuals.

How this study entered the registry

Interventions

This study administered: Prebiotic / dietary fibre supplement Design: randomized_controlled_trial. NUTRIOSE resistant dextrin fiber supplement, 6 weeks, vs control Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (abstract:35312171, route R4, confidence 0.55): “supplemented daily with either RD”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
