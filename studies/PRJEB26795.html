Archive description

To investigate the gut microbial characteristics of healthy Dutch children at 6-9 years as well as the relationship between their gut microbiota and multiple phenotypic factors, we collected stool samples from 281 children aged 6-9 years (mean age 7.25 years) enrolled in the KOALA Birth Cohort Study.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of child by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.85. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: Healthy Dutch children gut microbiome, KOALA birth cohort · population flags: healthy_volunteers.
