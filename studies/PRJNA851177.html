Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: randomized_controlled_trial. 4 weeks low-dose erythromycin or azithromycin in healthy adults; FMT of erythromycin-exposed mouse microbiota into germ-free mice Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (abstract:37347185, route R4, confidence 0.5): “healthy adults exposed to 4 weeks of low-dose erythromycin or azithromycin”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
