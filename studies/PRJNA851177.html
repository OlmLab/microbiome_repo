Gut microbiome in macrolide trial, Queensland

Alteration of the gut microbiome through long-term macrolide therapy and associated changes in systemic immune and metabolic regulation

40 faecal samples from healthy adults in Queensland, Australia, in an intervention study examining gut microbiome changes after long-term low-dose erythromycin or azithromycin therapy, with FMT used to test effects on host physiology. machine-written summary

Randomised controlled trialread from the paper

Stool samples from patients on long-term macrolide therapy (erythromycin or azithromycin) were compared at baseline and after antibiotic (ABX) exposure to assess gut microbiome changes. machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: Azithromycin, Baseline · Azithromycin, ABX · Erythromycin, Baseline · Erythromycin, ABX

In the data: 20 adults, all healthy; 20 sampled more than once (median 2, up to 2 samples per person), followed for a median of 28 days; sampled at: gut / stool.
