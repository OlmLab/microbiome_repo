Archive description

For this study, we included stool samples from healthy controls obtained from participants enrolled in the existing TB-DAR cohort, a prospective cohort of tuberculosis (TB) cases and their household contacts recruited between November 2013 and November 2015 at Temeke District Hospital in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania.
