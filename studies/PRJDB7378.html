Archive description

Total microbial community DNA was isolated from the fecal samples of Japanese patients who underwent colonoscopy at National Cancer Research Center. The study population contains health, gastrectomy.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.68. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: Japanese colonoscopy patients: healthy vs gastrectomy · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Tokyo Institute of Technology, first public 2019-12-09. Curated source gut_all_v1.
