Archive description

Shotgun metagenomic sequencing of fecal samples from patients with Crohn's disease before and after FMT to characterize fecal microbiome changes.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.82. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: Crohn's disease, before/after FMT · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre The Chinese University of Hong Kong, first public 2026-08-20. Curated source gut_all_v1.

Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: single_arm_before_after. FMT in Crohn's disease patients, before/after sampling Trial population: crohns_disease.

Evidence (bioproject.description, route R4, confidence 0.5): “before and after FMT to characterize fecal microbiome changes”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
