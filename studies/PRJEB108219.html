Stool samples from female breast cancer patients in Dubai, United Arab Emirates were collected longitudinally at multiple time points for full-length 16S rRNA gene sequencing and long-read whole metagenome sequencing. machine-written, checked

In the data: 17 people, all with other cancer; 14 sampled more than once (median 3, up to 3 samples per person), followed for a median of 4 months; sampled at: gut / stool.
