Archive description

The process that reabsorb calcium and phosphate from the bones instead of keeping these minerals just make our bones weaker, finally reach to the osteoporosis stage . The osteoporosis is a severe and common disease in elderly group, and it is the most common reason for fracture among the aged . Here we</p><p>carried out metagenomic shotgun sequencing to provide a detailed characterization of the compositional and functional features of gut microbiota change withe the bone loss .
