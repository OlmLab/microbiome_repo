Archive description

Stool microbiome of pediatric CDI patients receiving FMT procedure with adult donor material CDI patients receiving SOC therapy first to achieve C. difficile clearance prior to receiving FMT for gut microbiota reconstitution. This is shotgun metagenomic data.
