Archive description

The Antibiotics and Immune Responses (AIR) Clinical study will examine the effect of antibiotic exposure on immune responses to routine vaccinations given during the first six months of life. The study will consent participants shortly after birth with the aim of achieving enrolment of approximately 200 infants, with or without intrapartum/neonatal antibiotic exposure).

This study administered: Vaccination as the study intervention Design: observational_with_procedure. Routine infant vaccination (PCV13, 6-in-1 Infanrix Hexa); observed antibiotic exposure Trial population: healthy_control.
