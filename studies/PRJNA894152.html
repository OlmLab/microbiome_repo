Archive description

MinION long read sequencing data (Flongle & MinION flow cells) of fecal samples from infants and young children from rural Nicaragua. Household soil/dust and chicken fecal 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler and child by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic. Centre The Ohio State University, first public 2022-10-26.
