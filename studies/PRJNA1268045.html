Archive description

A total of 92 healthy subjects in Korea, each with quality-controlled, paired shotgun and amplicon sequencing data from stool samples, were selected through propensity score matching with the Korean NSCLC cohort based on age, BMI, and sex for comparison purposes.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by opus_adjudicated with confidence 0.85. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-opus-5-5 · health context: Healthy Korean subjects matched to NSCLC cohort · population flags: healthy_volunteers. Centre CJ Bioscience, Inc., first public 2025-05-28. Curated source gut_all_v1.
