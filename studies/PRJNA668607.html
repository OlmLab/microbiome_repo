Archive description

In this study, we used metagenomics sequencing of stool and swabs samples from symptomatic and asymptomatic patients infected with Vibrio cholerae, in order to study the within host evolution of the pathogen.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.75.
