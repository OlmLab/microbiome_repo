Archive description

Study was done to investigate the association between the gut microbiome of healthy individuals compared to individuals who are diagnosed with schizophrenia in the Malaysian population.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.68. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · population flags: disease_cohort;healthy_volunteers.
