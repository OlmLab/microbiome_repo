Archive description

Gut metagenomes of people treated for 30 days with a probiotic pasta containing spores of Alkalihalobacillus clausii

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.62. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: probiotic pasta intervention · population flags: antibiotic_trial;healthy_volunteers. Centre University of Naples Federico II, first public 2024-12-22. Curated source gut_all_v1.

Interventions

This study administered: Controlled / prescribed diet or feeding regimen Probiotic (live microorganisms; single strain or mixture) Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Probiotic pasta with Shouchella/Alkalihalobacillus clausii UBBC-07 spores, 80g/day, 4 weeks Trial population: obesity.

Evidence (abstract:41126806, route R4, confidence 0.55): “randomized controlled trial was conducted with 40 participants”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
