Preterm infant gut metagenomes, Pittsburgh NICU cohort

preterm infant gut metagenomes (S2_2013 and NIHY2_2012 cohorts)

407 longitudinal gut metagenome samples from preterm infants at Magee Womens Hospital, Pittsburgh, USA, characterizing colonization patterns; most samples are from NICU preterm infants (some with necrotizing enterocolitis) versus healthy controls. machine-written summary
