Preterm infant gut metagenomes from two cohorts (S2_2013 and NIHY2_2012) were sequenced to characterize colonization patterns in the preterm infant gut; no participant counts or comparison groups are stated. machine-written, checked

In the data: 407 neonates in 3 groups (Preterm / NICU 328; Necrotising enterocolitis 71; Healthy / control 8); one sample per person; sampled at: gut / stool.
