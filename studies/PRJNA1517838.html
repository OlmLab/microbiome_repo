Details
Archive description

This BioProject contains raw stool shotgun metagenomic sequencing reads from a prospective, open label, single arm longitudinal intervention study in Korean adults with obesity treated with short course metformin. Serial stool samples were collected at predefined study time points to characterize temporal changes in gut microbial taxonomic composition and functional potential. The goal of the study is to improve understanding of microbiome associated mechanisms of metformin response in obesity and to provide a resource for investigating gut microbiota dynamics, gut-liver axis biology, and candidate microbial biomarkers in human metabolic research.
