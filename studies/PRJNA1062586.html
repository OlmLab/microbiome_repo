Archive description

In a cross-sectional study of diabetic population, to explore the direct relationship between intestinal flora and blood glucose level, and to find new nutritional interventions targeting intestinal flora to assist in blood glucose control.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Sun Yat-Sen University, first public 2024-01-24.
