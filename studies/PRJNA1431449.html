Details
Archive description

This dataset contain 26 metagenomics sequencing files from 13 patients enrolled in a peanut allergy fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) trial. Samples were taken twice per patient at baseline and 4 months after the FMT. Please refer to a publication with the same title for more information.
