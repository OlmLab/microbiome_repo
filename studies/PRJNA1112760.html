Archive description

Nanopore sequencing reveals microbial features that are linked to the pathology of perinatal white-matter injury in extremely premature infants. Sequencing was performed at the Joint Microbiome Facility of the Medical University of Vienna and the University of Vienna under the project ID JMF-2103-08.
