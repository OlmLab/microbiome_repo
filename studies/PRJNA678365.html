Details
Archive description

Metagenomic (long-read) and 16S rRNA gene sequencing of humans, goats, and chickens in urban and rural Bangladesh. The goal of this work was to determine overlap in gut microbial communities between humans and common domestic animals (chickens and goats) and assess how this relationship differed in rural versus urban areas.
