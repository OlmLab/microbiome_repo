Archive description

Subjects were provided a commonly used probiotic interventions, 70 fecal samples from 26 patients collected before intervention, after intervention 4-week and 10-week were analyzed by metagenomic sequencing.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.60. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · population flags: antibiotic_trial;disease_cohort. Centre FMMU, first public 2023-10-03.
