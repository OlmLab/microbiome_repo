Archive description

The purpose of this study was to evaluate the efficacy of MaaT033 to (1) prevent the decay of the gut ecosystem (dysbiosis) to preserve immune homeostasis, (2) restore and optimize the gut ecosystem to full functionality including its role in repairing the gut epithelium and healthy gut barrier, and (3) maintain a restored gut ecosystem and fully functional immune homeostasis (ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier: NCT04150393).

Interventions

This study administered: Defined bacterial consortium / live biotherapeutic product Design: single_arm_before_after. MaaT033 pooled fecal microbiotherapy capsules after chemotherapy, phase 1b Trial population: other_cancer.

Evidence (abstract:40197991, route R4, confidence 0.55): “first-in-man use of MaaT033”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
