people with a condition compared with people without it

Fecal metagenomes from human hypertension patients and from wild-type mice after angiotensin perfusion, collected as stool samples in Beijing, China. machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: human hypertension group (n = 31) · human healthy group (n = 9) · mouse wild type after angiotensin perfusion

In the data: 40 samples from adults (which samples share a person is not recorded) in 2 groups (human hypertension group 31; human healthy group 9); sampled at: gut / stool.

Each circle: samples in one group at one body site (area ∝ count; hover for people and samples). Rows come from comparison_group, columns from body_site_code.
