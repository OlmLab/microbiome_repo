Study design
Cross-sectional comparison of groupsread from the paper
groups sampled once and compared

304 human stool samples from Winnipeg, Canada, collected 2015-2019, were analysed metagenomically to identify enteric pathogens; no comparison groups or sampling scheme are stated. machine-written, checked

In the data: 284 people, all with gi infection / diarrhoea; mostly one sample per person; 12 sampled more than once (median 2, up to 6 samples per person); sampled at: gut / stool.
