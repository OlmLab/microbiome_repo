Archive description

This project characterizes the impact of fidaxomicin (FDX) and lyophilized fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) capsules (EUTEGRA/MBK-01) on the gut microbiota of adult patients with recurrent Clostridioides difficile infection (rCDI), within the framework of the phase III ICD-01 clinical trial (EudraCT 2020-004591-17). Paired stool samples were collected at baseline (active infection) and eight weeks after therapeutic intervention from 27 patients (FDX, n=15 FMT, n=12). Shotgun metagenomic sequencing (Illumina NovaSeq X Plus) was performed on all samples to characterize microbiota taxonomic composition, alpha/beta diversity, functional pathways, C. difficile abundance and toxin-gene burden, and the fecal antibiotic-resistance gene reservoir. A subset of seven patients was additionally profiled by metatranscriptomics to assess the transcriptional activity of key microbial pathways. This dataset provides the raw paired-end shotgun metagenomic and metatranscriptomic reads underlying the comparison of the ecological and functional effects of an antibiotic-based versus a microbiota-based therapy for rCDI.
