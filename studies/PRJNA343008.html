Details
Archive description

It has been established that gut bacterial diversity rapidly increases after birth yet declines during old age however, little is known about the role of viral predation at these critical stages of life. This study will perform Virome comparison of infants and elderly to determine if an age delineated virome exists and to determine if birth mode has an effect on the virome.
