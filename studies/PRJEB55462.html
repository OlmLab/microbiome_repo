Archive description

306 shotgun metagenomic sequences from fecal samples of travelers. Samples were collected at 4 different timepoints, including before travel (T0), after travel (T1), during the first COVID19 pandemic wave in Hong Kong (T2) and the fifth pandemic wave (T3).
