Archive description

Metagenomes from patients participating in a randomised clinical trial receiving either FMT from donors or a placebo control.

How this study entered the registry

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: randomized_controlled_trial. FMT vs placebo FMT for TKI-induced diarrhoea in RCC patients Trial population: other_cancer.
