Archive description

Crohn's disease patients underwent one of two nutritional therapy regimes and were tracked for changes in their gut microbiome over the course of these treatments.

This study administered: Controlled / prescribed diet or feeding regimen Design: non_randomized_controlled. CDED+PEN or EEN nutritional therapy for 12 weeks in paediatric CD Trial population: crohns_disease.
