Details
Archive description

We performed a follow-up study of the gut microbiota in patients treated with antibiotic therapy, whom developed Clostridium difficile infection as a consequence. We used the 16s rRNA and metagenomic approaches to gain insights in the effect of the pathogen infection in the structure and functions of the human gut microbiota.
