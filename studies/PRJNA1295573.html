Archive description

This study used metagenomic sequencing on stool samples collected from 278 patients with pre-cancerous DCIS or early-stage invasive breast cancer. We compared the baseline stool microbiomes of subjects with early vs invasive breast cancer, and across invasive BC subtypes, prior to any cancer treatment.
