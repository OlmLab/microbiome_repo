Archive description

Shotgun metagenomic stool samples for the AtezoTRIBE phase-II randomized trial which was established to study the impact of the atezolizumab immune therapy treatment to folfoxiri plus bevacizumab treatment in unresectable metastatic colorectal cancer (CRC) patients.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre University of Trento, first public 2025-01-15.
