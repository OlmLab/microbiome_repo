Details
Archive description

Shotgun metagenomic stool samples from CRC patients enrolled at Masaryk Memorial Cancer Institute (Brno, Czech Republic) from 2015 to 2019. This cohort was established to study the microbiome-CRC link at different stages of the adenoma-carcinoma sequences as part of the ONCOBIOME consortium.
