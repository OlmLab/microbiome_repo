Archive description

Shotgun metagenomic stool samples from CRC patients enrolled at Masaryk Memorial Cancer Institute (Brno, Czech Republic) from 2015 to 2019. This cohort was established to study the microbiome-CRC link at different stages of the adenoma-carcinoma sequences as part of the ONCOBIOME consortium.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre University of Trento, first public 2025-01-23.
