Details
Archive description

Vaginal and endocervical metagenomes of Pacific Islanders from healthy and Chlamydia trachomatis positive women. These metagenomes are an extension to the existing repertoire of vaginal and endocervical metagenomes from various ethnic backgrounds to better understand the role of cervicovaginal microbiome in health and disease.
