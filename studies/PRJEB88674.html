Nasopharyngeal microbiome in COVID-19/PASC and flu, Belgium

Association of nasopharyngeal Dolosigranulum pigrum and Corynebacterium species with post-acute sequelae of SARS-CoV-2 (PASC) in a longitudinal cohort

Longitudinal shotgun metagenomics of 235 nasopharyngeal swabs from 156 participants (healthy, influenza, and moderate/severe COVID-19 patients) in Belgium, sampled during acute infection and ~3-month follow-up to study microbiome links to PASC. machine-written summary
