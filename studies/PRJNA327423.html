Gut and oral virome in cohabitating adults, USA

Campus Antibiotics Virome Study

Shotgun metagenomic sequencing of 192 gut and oral virome specimens from 20 cohabitating, genetically unrelated household contacts in the USA, collected 2014-2015 to study bacteriophage sharing and transmission. machine-written summary
