Details
Archive description

Pathologic changes associated with AUD have been described in both the brain and gut microbiome, but exact mechanisms underlying altered gut-brain signaling with patients with AUD are still unknown. This study examined associations between the gut microbiome, brain morphometry from structural MRI images and clinical symptoms in treatment-seeking individuals with AUD.
