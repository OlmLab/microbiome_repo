Archive description

The purpose of this study is to determine if supplementing healthy term infants delivered by C-section or vaginal delivery who only consume breastmilk with a probiotic for 21 consecutive days increases levels of bacteria in infants' stool.

Interventions

This study administered: Probiotic (live microorganisms; single strain or mixture) Design: non_randomized_controlled. Bifidobacterium infantis EVC001 fed daily day 7-28 of life vs no probiotic Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (abstract:29242832, route R4, confidence 0.55): “fed B. infantis EVC001 to their infants from day 7 to day”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
