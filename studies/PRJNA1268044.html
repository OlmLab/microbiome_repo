Archive description

Stool samples for both shotgun and amplicon sequencing were collected at screening (n = 92), after C2D1 (n = 82), and at progression (n = 36) to identify factors associated with ICI response.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.75.
