Archive description

The Southern Community Cohort Study (SCCS) is an ongoing prospective cohort study, with the aim to investigate risk factors of cancer and other chronic diseases across 12 southeastern U.S. states: Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, Louisiana, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Virginia, and West Virginia. Approximately 86,000 adults, two-thirds Black people, between 40 and 79 years of age were enrolled from 2002 to 2009. Approximately 86% of the participants were recruited from community health centers (CHCs) and institutions providing basic health care and preventative services in underserved areas, so the cohort encompassed a substantial number of individuals characterized by low income, low education, and lack of insurance.
