Archive description

Shotgun metagenomics sequencing of stool samples of French patients with C. difficile infection (CDI) before (after antibiotics) and after FMT + donors samples.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.72. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: C. difficile infection patients undergoing FMT · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Saint-Antoine Research Center;CRSA, first public 2026-09-06. Curated source gut_all_v1.

Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: single_arm_before_after. Antibiotics then FMT for C. difficile infection, sampled before/after Trial population: gi_infection_or_diarrhoea.

Evidence (bioproject.description, route R4, confidence 0.5): “before (after antibiotics) and after FMT”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
