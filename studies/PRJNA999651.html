Archive description

Stool of hospitalized patients was collected and sequenced in order to look at the prevalence and spread of antibiotic resistance genes within the gut microbiome.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.78.
