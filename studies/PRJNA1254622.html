Details
Archive description

Total neoadjuvant therapy (TNT) is standard for locally advanced rectal cancer, yet treatment response is biologically heterogeneous, underscoring the need for predictive biomarkers. It is aimed to characterize how baseline and on-treatment gut microbiome ecology relate to clinically relevant response dimensions, and to identify baseline-level markers that could be integrated into a pre-treatment response score.
