Archive description

Metagenomic (llumina) sequencing of human fecal samples from rural Kenya. These samples were combined with others from around the world to assess the association between access to sanitation and antibiotic resistance carriage in human guts.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and elderly by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.75. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: sanitation access and antibiotic resistance carriage · population flags: other. Centre Tufts University, first public 2022-02-02. Curated source gut_all_v1.
