Details
Archive description

Long COVID (LC) is a debilitating condition that can follow acute SARS-CoV-2 infection. It manifests differently across individuals, but the most severe cases are often associated with neuroinflammation and neuropsychological sequelae. It is known that dysbiosis and leaky gut are also associated with this disease
