Details
Archive description

Stool samples collected from N=60 NYU Langone Health and N=36 Yale New Haven Hospital adult patients with laboratory-confirmed SARS-CoV-2 infection bacterial DNA extraction with Qiagen PowerSoil Pro kit on the QiaCube HT samples prepared for 16S rRNA gene sequencing by using KAPA HiFi polymerase to amplify the V4 region of the 16S rRNA gene and sequenced on Illumina MiSeq using paired-end 2x250 reads and MiSeq Reagent Kitv2 samples prepared for shotgun metagenomic sequencing with a procedure adapted from the Nextera Library Prep kit (Illumina), and sequenced on an Illumina NovaSeq using paired-end 2x150 reads (Illumina) aiming for 100M read depth
