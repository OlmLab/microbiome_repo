Gut microbiome of preschool children in rural Zimbabwe

Fecal microbiomes from a Zimbabwean pre-school population

Archive description

Fecal samples were obtained from 116 pre-school children in Zimbabwe, and the DNA content assessed using paired-end sequencing.
