Interventions

This study administered: Controlled / prescribed diet or feeding regimen Non-microbial dietary supplement (vitamins Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Oral sodium butyrate 20 mg/kg/day vs placebo, plus standard calorie-restriction care, 6 months Trial population: obesity.

Evidence (abstract:36469320, route R4, confidence 0.55): “supplemented with oral sodium butyrate”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
