Archive description

The goal of this study is to describe the profile of gut microbiome in adults with and without UC and to investigate the association between microbial composition and UC in China using fecal WGS.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: disease_cohort. Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: single_arm_before_after. Capsulized fecal microbiota transplantation in active ulcerative colitis patients Trial population: ulcerative_colitis.

Evidence (study.title, route R4, confidence 0.5): “received capsulized fecal microbiota transplantation”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
