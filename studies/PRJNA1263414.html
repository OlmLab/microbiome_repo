Archive description

For a correlative study on the effect of the composition of intestinal microbiota on the efficacy of immunotherapy using ICI, 62 pre-treatment fecal samples from adult melanoma patients were recruited.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and elderly by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.85. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: melanoma patients undergoing immunotherapy · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Lopukhin FRCC PCM, first public 2025-06-23. Curated source gut_all_v1.
