Details
Archive description

Whole genome sequencing analysis of fecal samples collected from people living with HIV. Aim to identify microbial taxa and functional pathways that correlate with the size of the intact HIV reservoir, with the goal of uncovering novel microbiome-based targets for reservoir modulation.
