Archive description

This dataset includes gut microbiome features and associated metabolomics data from children with Kawasaki disease (KD) in Northwest China. The primary research aim is to characterize the gut microbial composition, metabolic profiles, and their potential associations in pediatric KD within this geographical region, providing integrated baseline data to explore the interplay between gut microbiota, metabolites, and KD pathogenesis, as well as to identify potential microbial and metabolic markers for clinical research.
