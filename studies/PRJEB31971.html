Archive description

In this study we subjected ancient human remains (coprolites, large intestine content, and soft tissue) to high throughput shotgun seqeuncing and metagenomics analysis.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.65.
