Archive description

Human fecal samples from hydatid patients, liver cirrhosis patients, and healthy controls (shortgun). Paired oral and fecal samples from patients with hydatid disease and cirrhosis (full-length 16S). Transcriptomes of S. anginosus cultures and liver tissues from Echinococcus-infected mice (bulk RNA-seq).

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.57. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · population flags: disease_cohort;healthy_volunteers. Centre Lanzhou Veterinary Research Institute, first public 2025-10-01. Curated source gut_all_v1.
