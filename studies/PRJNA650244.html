Archive description

Stool samples were collected from in-hospital COVID-19 patients to characterise gut microbiome community composition associated with this disease.

How this study entered the registry

Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Chinese University of Hong Kong, first public 2021-01-12.
