Details
Archive description

This study provides 16S rRNA and metagenomic shotgun sequencing of fecal samples from gastrointestinal cancer patients receiving anti-PD-1 immunotherapy. The goals are to investigate this association in gastrointestinal (GI) tract malignancies and to identify commensal bacterial taxa that can be potentially used for patient stratification.

How this project entered the registry
