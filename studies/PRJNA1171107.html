Archive description

Metagenomic sequencing of stool from capecitabine-treated colorectal cancer patients, collected at baseline (t1), during cycle 3 (t2), and post-treatment (t3).

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.80.
