Archive description

The goal of the study was to measure Proteobacteria, obligate anaerobes, butyrate-producers, and antimicrobial resistant genes after therapy with a microbial consortia or FMT for recurrent Clostridioides difficile infection.

Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Defined bacterial consortium / live biotherapeutic product Design: non_randomized_controlled. Microbial consortium (MET-2) vs FMT for recurrent C. difficile infection Trial population: gi_infection_or_diarrhoea.

Evidence (abstract:37404011, route R4, confidence 0.55): “microbial consortium, microbial ecosystem therapeutic (MET-2), and FMT”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
