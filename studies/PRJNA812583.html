Archive description

We aim to assess if fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) during pregnancy is effective and safe. Furthermore, we aim to track the strain transmission from the donor, to the mother, and further to the infant.

How this study entered the registry

Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: single_arm_before_after. FMT via colonoscopy for CDI during pregnancy Trial population: gi_infection_or_diarrhoea.

Evidence (abstract:36352460, route R4, confidence 0.55): “treated with FMT via colonoscopy”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
