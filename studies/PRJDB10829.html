Archive description

Total microbial community DNA was isolated from the fecal samples of Japanese patients who underwent colonoscopy at National Cancer Research Center. The study population contains CRC and postoperative patients.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.75.
