Archive description

The aim of the study was to analyzed the fecal virome of children in two small uncontacted populations and compare them to those of age-matched children from a more connected Venezuelan village and the city of Caracas.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of child and neonate and infant / toddler and adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.60.
