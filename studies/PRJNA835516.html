Archive description

Sixty-five faecal samples, including 50 from each of individuals at the time of fungal cultivation and 15 longitudinal samples over three years later were performed for whole-metagenome shotgun sequencing.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.85. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · population flags: healthy_volunteers. Centre Dalian Medical University, first public 2022-05-08.
