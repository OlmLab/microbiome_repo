Resistome of healthy Swiss adults' stool, cross-sectional

Resistome and microbiome analysis of stool samples from healthy people using shotgun metagenomics.

88 stool samples from healthy adults in Switzerland were analyzed by shotgun metagenomics to assess gut resistome and colonization with extended-spectrum cephalosporin-resistant Enterobacterales, comparing native versus pre-enriched sample processing in a cross-sectional design. machine-written summary
