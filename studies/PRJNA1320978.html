Dark chocolate, cocoa, and almonds influence gut microbiome in adults with overweight or obesity

Archive description

4-period (4-wk/period) controlled-feeding crossover trial in adults with overweight or obesity: Participants (n=21) consumed similar diets containing: 42.5 g of raw almonds (ALD, 253kcal/d), 18 g of cocoa and 43 g of dark chocolate (CHOC, 251kcal/d), both (CHOC+ALD, 504kcal/d), or an average American diet (AAD). Study foods replaced an isocaloric amount of butter, cheese, and refined grains in the AAD. Stool samples were collected pre-trial and after each test diet. Stool microbiota were identified by 16S sequencing.
