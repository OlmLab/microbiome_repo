Archive description

Previous studies have compared the gut microbiota between athletes and sedentary individuals, but the influence of diverse training patterns on the gut microbiota of professional athletes re-mains unclear. This study aimed to explore these differences among professional athletes from road cycling, handball, shooting, and weightlifting, and a control group of university students. Stool samples from 69 professional athletes and 22 controls were analyzed, revealing significant differences in microbiota composition, function, and metabolic phenotypes.
