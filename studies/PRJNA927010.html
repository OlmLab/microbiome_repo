Archive description

The PREVAIL term birth cohort aims to characterize the first two years of life of mother-infant dyads. Metagenomic sequencing was performed to address questions of delivery, genetic predisposition and disease progression within the infants and the impact of nutrition on the developing gut.

How this study entered the registry
