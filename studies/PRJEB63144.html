Details
Archive description

The skin microbiome of atopic dermatitis patients and healthy controls was studies by collecting and analyzing skin swabs. Metagenomic shotgun sequencing was used to compare overall skin microbiome diversity and differential abundance analysis of specific species between the different groups.
