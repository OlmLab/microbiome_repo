Archive description

Novaseq reads derived from fecal samples of 29 Japanese healthy participants, 62 patients with relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis, and 15 patients with secondary progressive multiple sclerosis are deposited. Sequel II HiFi reads derived from fecal samples of 2 patients with relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis, and 1 patient with secondary progressive multiple sclerosis are also deposited.
