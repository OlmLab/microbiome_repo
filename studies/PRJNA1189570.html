Faecal virome mock-spike method comparison, UK

Bias and reproducibility of viral metagenomics methods for the combined detection of faecal RNA and DNA viruses

24 faecal/control specimens from 3 human donors in the UK, spiked with a mock virus community at two concentrations and processed by WTA2 or SISPA, sequenced by combined DNA/RNA metagenomics to assess method bias and reproducibility. machine-written summary
