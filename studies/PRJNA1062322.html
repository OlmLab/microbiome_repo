Details
Archive description

Dietary supplements administered to preterm infants in neonatal intensive care units have the potential to influence the intestinal microbiome composition which may affect overall health status of the infant. Our study reports changes to the intestinal microbiome associated with administration of human milk cream a novel supplement used to improve growth rates of preterm very low birth weight infants.
