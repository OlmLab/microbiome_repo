Archive description

The raw sequencing reads of 18 samples of human feces with a 24-hours batch culture in vitro under phage cocktail treatment or heat-killed phage treatment.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.65. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: in vitro phage cocktail treatment of feces · population flags: other. Centre Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, first public 2023-01-11. Curated source gut_all_v1.

Interventions

This study administered: Bacteriophage therapy Design: non_randomized_controlled. In vitro fecal batch culture with phage cocktail vs heat-killed phage

Evidence (bioproject.description, route R4, confidence 0.5): “under phage cocktail treatment or heat-killed phage treatment”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
