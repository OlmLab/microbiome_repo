Details
Archive description

The project is interested in characterizing the microbiome and resistome of dairy workers,</p><p>whose exposure to the livestock farming environment places them at risk for</p><p>facilitating community transmission of antibiotic resistant genes and emerging</p><p>zoonotic diseases. Using shotgun metagenomics sequencing, we investigated differences in the taxonomy,</p><p>diversity and gene presence of the human gut microbiome of 10 dairy farm</p><p>workers and 6 community controls, with a focus on antibiotic resistance genes</p><p>and virulence factors in the occupationally-exposed cohort.
