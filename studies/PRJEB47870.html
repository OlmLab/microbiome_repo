Respiratory metagenomes in COVID-19 patients, Canada

Analysis of diversity, co-pathogens, and metagenomics of COVID-19 patients

Shotgun metagenomic and metatranscriptomic sequencing of 250 nasopharyngeal and throat swabs from 125 individuals in Canada, comparing SARS-CoV-2 positive/negative, symptomatic/asymptomatic status, and co-pathogen detection, collected in 2021. machine-written summary
