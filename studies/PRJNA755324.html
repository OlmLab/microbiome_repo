Archive description

VE303 isolated bacterial genome sequences and stool metagenomes from the VE303 phase 1 healthy volunteer study conducted by Vedanta Biosciences, Inc.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.78. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: Live biotherapeutic product trial in healthy adults · population flags: healthy_volunteers. Interventions

This study administered: Antibiotic given as the study intervention (incl. eradication therapy Defined bacterial consortium / live biotherapeutic product Design: randomized_controlled_trial. VE303 (8-strain LBP) dosed after vancomycin pretreatment, phase 1a/b Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (abstract:35421353, route R4, confidence 0.55): “VE303 strains optimally colonize HVs if dosed over multiple days after vancomycin”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
