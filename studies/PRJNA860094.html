Archive description

we reported the clinical and immunological characteristics of patients with T. marneffei infection in a Chinese cohort and emphasized the application value of mNGS in the diagonosis of T. marneffei infection. We aimed to improve the early recognition of T. marneffei infection in IEI patients.
