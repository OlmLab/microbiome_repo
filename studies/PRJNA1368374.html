Details
Archive description

This study encompasses a cohort with diverse maternal conditions, including healthy mothers, with HIV infection, and with HBV infection. Metagenomic data are available from fecal samples of these mothers. For infants, longitudinal metagenomic data from fecal samples are available during early life (at birth, 1 month, and 3 months).
