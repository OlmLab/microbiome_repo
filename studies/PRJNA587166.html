Urinary virome in kidney transplant recipients with BK viremia

Virome and Microbiome in Healthy and BK disease of Kidney Transplant Recipients

Longitudinal shotgun metagenomics of 65 urine specimens from 23 adult/elderly kidney transplant recipients (11 BKV+, 12 BKV-) characterizing the urinary virome over 14 weeks post-transplant. machine-written summary
