Archive description

V4 16S rRNA amplicon (n=207) and metagenomic shotgun (n=161) of human gut communities with logitudinal sampling before and after very low calorie diet. V4 16S rRNA amplicons (n=77) of human donors and recipient gnotobiotic mice.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.72. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: diet perturbation, very low calorie diet · population flags: disease_cohort;healthy_volunteers. This study administered: Controlled / prescribed diet or feeding regimen Design: single_arm_before_after. Very-low-calorie diet, longitudinal before/after sampling Trial population: obesity.
