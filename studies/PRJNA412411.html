Very-low-calorie diet effects on human gut microbiota

Diet-induced perturbations to the human gut microbiome

160 samples from obese individuals in Berlin, Germany, collected before and after very-low-calorie diet intervention. Study includes 16S rRNA amplicon and metagenomic shotgun sequencing with transplantation experiments in gnotobiotic mice. machine-written summary

Archive description

V4 16S rRNA amplicon (n=207) and metagenomic shotgun (n=161) of human gut communities with logitudinal sampling before and after very low calorie diet. V4 16S rRNA amplicons (n=77) of human donors and recipient gnotobiotic mice.
