Archive description

Infections remain an important cause of morbidity and mortality in pediatricallogeneic hematopoietic cell transplantation (HCT) recipients. Disruption of the gut microbiota has been linked to poor outcomes in single-center studies of these patients, however application to other centers has been limited. This multi-center study aimed to evaluate drivers of multi drug resistant organism gene dynamics in pediatric HCT recipients across multiple centers in the United States and Europe.
