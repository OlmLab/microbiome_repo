Archive description

Data generated from a thorough characterization of the host and gut microbiome response to fecal transplant in a small cohort of ulcerative colitis patients. The aims were to establish the downstream effects of fecal transplant on the gut microbiome, stool and blood metabolome, and the local and systemic immune system.

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Colonoscopy FMT induction + 12wk daily encapsulated oral FMT vs sham Trial population: ulcerative_colitis.
