Archive description

This study examined pediatric patients undergoing treatment for leukemia and lymphoma. It examined how age, antibiotic use and antifungal use impacted the microbiome during treatment.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of child and infant / toddler by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
