Archive description

Shotgun sequencing of 0.8 micron filtered and nuclease-digested viral fraction from human feces with and without NAFLD. Both viral DNA and RNA (via cDNA) was sequenced on Illumina platforms. Human reads were removed using Kneaddata and Pathseqs.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.70.
