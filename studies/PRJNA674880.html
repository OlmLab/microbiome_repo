Archive description

This sequence repository contains longitudinal fecal metagenomes from 29 human adults with recurrent C. difficile infection after treatment with a microbiome-based transplant product labeled as RBX2660, which was created from the stool of healthy screened donors. The metagenomes were sequenced by 16S rRNA amplification and by whole genome shotgun sequencing. Additionally, 38 antimicrobial drug resistant isolates were selectively cultured from these metagenomes, and their whole genome shotgun sequences along with 16S sequences are provided.

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: single_arm_before_after. RBX2660 microbiota-based FMT, 1 or 2 doses, recurrent CDI Trial population: gi_infection_or_diarrhoea.

Evidence (abstract:33593430, route R4, confidence 0.55): “effects of RBX2660, a microbiota-based investigational therapeutic”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
