Archive description

Cross-sectional study of the microbial composition and resistance profiles of the gut microbiome of individuals with liver disease. The project was made of four cohorts: healthy controls, stable cirrhotic, decompensated cirrhotic and acute-on-chronic liver failure.

How this study entered the registry
