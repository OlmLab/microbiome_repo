Details
Archive description

In this study we have sequenced the virome in longitudinal plasma samples of chronic hepatitis B virus (HBV) infected patients. Patients were diagnosed in different stages of chronic HBV infection. The observed virome dynamics were not associated with the different stages. We did find a high individuality in the oberved virome of the patients.
