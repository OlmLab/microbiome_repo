Gut microbiome in a probiotic trial in malnourished elderly, China

Quantitative multiomics analysis reveals the effect of Clostridium butyricum on elderly people with malnutrition

Randomized single-blind 12-week trial of Clostridium butyricum supplementation in 19 malnourished elderly adults in long-term care in China, with 63 stool specimens profiled by shotgun metagenomic sequencing. machine-written summary

Study design
Randomised controlled trialread from the paper
people randomly assigned to arms

Elderly malnourished long-term care residents in Shanghai were randomised to a 12-week Clostridium butyricum probiotic intervention (TG) or no-probiotic control (CG), with fecal and plasma samples collected before, after 12 weeks, and 12 weeks post-discontinuation. machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: Test group (TG) - baseline (T0) (n = 11) · Test group (TG) - after 12 weeks probiotic (T3) (n = 11) · Test group (TG) - 12 weeks post-discontinuation (T6) (n = 11) · Control group (CG) - baseline (C0) (n = 8) · Control group (CG) - after 12 weeks (C3) (n = 8) · Control group (CG) - 12 weeks later (C6) (n = 8)

In the data: 63 samples from older adults (which samples share a person is not recorded), all with malnutrition / undernutrition; sampled at: gut / stool.
