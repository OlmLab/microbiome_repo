NGS-Based Detection of Bloodstream Pathogens and AMR Genes in Sepsis Patients Using PISTETM Workflow

Prospective multicenter study of 7 blood specimens from adult sepsis patients in Athens, Greece (2023-2024), using combined 16S rRNA gene and metagenomic DNA sequencing (PISTE workflow) to detect bloodstream pathogens versus standard blood cultures. machine-written summary

Details
Archive description

Prospective, multicenter phase IIa study evaluating the diagnostic performance of PISTE technology, a next-generation sequencing (NGS) workflow combining full-length 16S rRNA gene sequencing with metagenomic analysis, in comparison to standard-of-care (SoC) cultures for the rapid diagnosis of circulating bacteria in blood in patients with suspected sepsis.
