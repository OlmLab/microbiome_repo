Archive description

Cohort of spanish mother and infants across multiple delivery mode and perinatal conditions: place (hospital, home), mode of delivery (vaginal, cesarean), breastfeeding and its duration. For the mothers, stool and milk shotgun metagenomics samples are available. For the infant, longitidinal stool shotgun metagenomics samples are available across the first year of age (1 week, 1 month, 6 months, 12 months)

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool / human milk / vaginal / urogenital shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler and adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
