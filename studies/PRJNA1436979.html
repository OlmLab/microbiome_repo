Archive description

This project investigated gut microbial alterations in human fecal samples from Normal, MPEI, and SPEI groups using shotgun metagenomic sequencing. Total DNA was extracted from stool samples, paired-end libraries were prepared, and sequencing was performed on the Illumina NovaSeq X Plus platform.
