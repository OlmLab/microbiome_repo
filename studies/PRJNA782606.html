Archive description

The gut microbiota at time of vaccination could impact the development of immune responses to oral cholera vaccine. The goal of this study is to identify bacterial species or strains that are related to long-term memory B cell responders and non-responders.

This study administered: Vaccination as the study intervention Design: observational_with_procedure. Oral cholera vaccine administration, pre-vaccination fecal microbiota sampled Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (abstract:41388019, route R4, confidence 0.55): “immune responses after OCV using a reference-independent gene-level method”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
