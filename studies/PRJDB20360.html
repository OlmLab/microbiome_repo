Archive description

This is an observational/translational study to evaluate clinical outcomes of nivolumab and to discover novel immune-related biomarkers (gut microbiome, genetic polymorphism, gene expression and metabolome in plasma) in gastric cancer, using fecal and blood samples at two points before and after treatment.
