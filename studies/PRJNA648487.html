Details
Archive description

This study focused on identifying associations between early-life dietary nutrition, perinatal medical environments, feeding mode, and socioeconomic factors, and the gut microbiome of MLPT babies. We also investigated if longitudinal changes in gut microbial structure from D10 to 4M were associated with infant growth. Our results aim to provide an insights to optimise the health outcomes for MLPT babies.
