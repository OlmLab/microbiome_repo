Archive description

Comparison of gut microbiome between ASD children and neurotypical children. We characterized compositional alterations in gut microbiome in ASD and identified novel fecal bacterial markers for ASD.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler and child by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
