Archive description

Samples describe the fecal microbiome of a human cohort with persistent positive COVID-19 disease and a group of COVID-19 participants with a viral load duration considered to be within normal time range (controls). The fecal samples are studied by metagenomic analysis through a shotgun approach.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.78. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: persistent positive COVID-19 vs controls · population flags: disease_cohort.
