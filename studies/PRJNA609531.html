Details
Archive description

The diagnosis of bacterial pathogens in lower respiratory tract infections (LRIs) using conventional culture methods remains challenging and time-consuming. To evaluate the clinical performance of a rapid nanopore-sequencing based metagenomics test for diagnosis of bacterial pathogens in major LRIs through a large-scale prospective near patient study.
