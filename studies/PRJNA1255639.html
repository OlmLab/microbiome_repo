Archive description

Critically ill patients, including those with systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS) and sepsis, often show altered gut microbiota due to physiological stress and the use of broad-spectrum antibiotics. Although antibiotics are essential for treating infections, they can disrupt the gut microbial balance, potentially worsening patient outcomes. Understanding these changes may help guide microbiota-based interventions to reduce antibiotic-associated harm. This study aimed to identify specific bacterial taxa that distinguish sepsis from non-sepsis cases. A total of 89 stool samples (51 non-sepsis, 38 sepsis) were analyzed to assess microbial diversity and detect differentially abundant taxa using 16S rRNA gene sequencing.
