Archive description

Here, we provide matched 16S,ITS,Metagenome and Metatranscriptome data obtained from CDI- and CDI+ diarrheal samples.

How this project entered the catalog

The registry (archive-only classification) lists it as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown. In the catalog: 75 DNA and 27 RNA shotgun sequencing runs (amplicon and single-cell runs are left out run by run).
