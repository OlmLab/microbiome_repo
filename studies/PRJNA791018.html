Archive description

Different impact of heat-inactivated and viable Lactobacillus (Lactiplantibacillus) plantarum TWK10 on exercise performance.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.60. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: exercise performance, probiotic supplementation · population flags: healthy_volunteers. Centre SYNBIO TECH INC., first public 2022-12-22. Curated source gut_all_v1.

Interventions

This study administered: Probiotic (live microorganisms; single strain or mixture) Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Viable vs heat-killed L. plantarum TWK10, 6 weeks, 3x10^11 CFU/day Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (abstract:36363775, route R4, confidence 0.55): “randomly divided into three groups: Control, TWK10”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
