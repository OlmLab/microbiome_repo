Archive description

The prophylactic application of doxycycline is one option used for antimalarial chemoprophylaxis by German soldiers on tropical deployments. In a case-control-assessment, diagnostic metagenomics was applied to comparatively assess antimicrobial resistance genes in enteric microbiomes of soldiers with and without medical history of doxycycline-based antimalarial chemoprophylaxis on deployment.
