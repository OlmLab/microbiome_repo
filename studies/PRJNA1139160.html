Details
Archive description

This is a randomized, quasi-blinded, placebo-controlled, mono-centric pilot trial investigating the feasibility of a main trial regarding the effects of a lyophilized lysate of E. coli strains for preventing urinary tract infections in individuals with spinal cord injury in their primary rehabilitation.
