Details
Archive description

Evaluation of vaccines against typhoid and other enteric pathogens may be informed by better understanding the relationship between the effector immune response and the gut microbiota. Longitudinal fecal samples were collected from healthy adults recruited in Oxford, UK. Volunteers received 3 doses
