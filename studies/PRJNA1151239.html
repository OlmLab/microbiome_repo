Details
Archive description

A multicenter cross-sectional study was conducted in Xiamen, Shanghai, and Nanjing in China from October 2022 to March 2023. A total of 335 couples of fecal samples were collected and analyzed for gut microbiota analysis. The gut microbiota was analyzed by 16S rRNA gene sequencing and metagenomic sequencing.
