Details
Archive description

Information on the human placental virome is currently limited, and the association between subclinical placental viral infections and spontaneous preterm birth (PTB) remains poorly understood. In this study, we examined fresh placental samples from women in the UK, Bangladesh, and South Africa to characterise virome profiles and assess their association with spontaneous PTB. Shotgun metagenomic sequencing was performed on villous and decidual tissue samples using Oxford Nanopore Technologies. Viral DNA and total RNA were extracted separately from the tissues. RNA was transcribed into dsDNA and then combined with viral DNA prior to library preparation using the Oxford Nanopore Technologies Rapid PCR Barcoding Kit. Individual sample libraries were barcoded, pooled in groups of 12, and sequenced using R9.4.1 flow cells on the GridION MK1 platform. Taxonomic classification was conducted using Kraken2 against the Viral and Standard-8 databases independently. The abundance of each taxon was estimated to the species level using Bracken v2.8.
