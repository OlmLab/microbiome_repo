Archive description

Infant gut metagenomes collected by the Khula cohort (South Africa) as part of the Wellcome LEAP 1kD grant to investigate relationships between the gut microbiome and developmental outcomes in the first 1000 days of life

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler and neonate by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic. Centre Vanja Klepac-Ceraj, first public 2025-01-04.
