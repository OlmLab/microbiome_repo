Archive description

We studied the microbiome of patients undergoing and recovering from hematopoietic cell transplantation (HCT). These data were include standard short-read metagenomic sequencing, as well as "linked-read" sequencing libraries prepared on the 10X Genomics Chromium. This dataset is linked to a study of transmission of pathogens in the microbiome of HCT patients.
