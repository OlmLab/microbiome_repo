Details
Archive description

The study investigates the potential transmission of the human microbiome members from mothers to infants using shotgun metagenomics. We targeted 25 mother/infant pairs, considering multiple bodysides (stool, tongue dorsum, vagina and skin).

How this project entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool / oral cavity / skin / vaginal / urogenital shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler and adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Assay shotgun_dna.
