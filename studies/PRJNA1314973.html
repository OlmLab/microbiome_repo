Details
Archive description

This study characterizes the human plasma virome and immune markers in young (0-16 years) and elderly (63-100 years) cohorts. Blood samples from the elderly cohort were collected at Taizhou Fourth Peoples Hospital between March and July 2023, and samples from the young cohort were collected at Shanghai Childrens Hospital between August and November 2024. Plasma and serum fractions were separated, and serum was used to measure HERV-K envelope protein levels. Plasma samples were treated to remove free nucleic acids, and total DNA and RNA was extracted, reverse-transcribed, and amplified. Metagenomic libraries were prepared and sequenced on the Illumina NovaSeq platform to generate high-depth paired-end reads. Viral reads were assembled, quality-checked, and annotated to generate a dataset representing circulating blood viruses across age groups, providing resources for studying virome composition and its association with immune markers and age-related conditions.
