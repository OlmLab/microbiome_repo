Details
Archive description

We conducted integrative metagenomic and metabolomic analyses of fecal and blood samples in a cohort of women with gestational diabetes mellitus and pregnant women with normal glucose tolerance to identify GDM-associated signatures of fecal microorganisms and metabolites.
