Details
Archive description

The infant gut microbiome matures rapidly in early life, yet species-level longitudinal data remain limited. We performed metagenomic sequencing on 921 stool samples from 209 Latino infants living in Southern California across five timepoints, 1-month, 6-months, 12-months, 18-months and 24-months.
