Details
Archive description

Our pilot study analyzed a total of 34 samples collected from five patients diagnosed with Basal Cell Carcinoma (BCC). All participants are white males. Except for one participant, who provided one BCC sample and one perilesional sample, each subject contributed two samples each of BCC, perilesional, sun-exposed, and non-sun-exposed types.
