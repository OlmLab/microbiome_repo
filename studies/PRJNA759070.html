Gut metagenomics in GH-secreting pituitary adenoma

metagenomic sequencing in patients with GHPA (growth hormone secreting pituitary adenoma)

19 gut metagenome samples from a case-control study comparing patients with GH-secreting pituitary adenoma and healthy controls, analyzed by shotgun metagenomic sequencing for species-level taxonomic and functional differences. machine-written summary
