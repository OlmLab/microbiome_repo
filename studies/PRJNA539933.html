Details
Archive description

The aims of this project were to compare the gut microbiome and resistome between sympatric wild chimpanzees and gorillas in the Republic of the Congo, captive apes living in zoos in the United States, and humans living near the wild apes in the Congo.

How this project entered the registry

Classified as human and non-human (mixed) gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.62. Assay shotgun_dna.
