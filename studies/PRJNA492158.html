Archive description

Results on the difference of gut microbial composition between vegetarians and omnivores are inconclusive and little is known about how such potential differences are translated to active functionalities. We aimed to characterize the gut microbiota composition, gut microbiota-host cometabolites and microbial functionalities of individuals consuming vegetarian diets.
