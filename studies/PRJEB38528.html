Archive description

Placenta biopsies (16S), and saliva samples, vaginal and rectal swabs (shotgun) collected at delivery for 76 women delivering at term, 50 by planned C-section and 26 vaginally. Also includes 16S for bacterial colonies cultured from placenta biopsies.
