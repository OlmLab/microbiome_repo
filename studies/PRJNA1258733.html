Archive description

Shotgun metagenomics data from a unique longitudinal study of ten rural infants in Morelos, Mexico, sampled monthly from birth to 18 months, profiling bacterial gut microbiomes to explore early-life microbial development in a rural environment.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
