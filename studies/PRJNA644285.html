Details
Archive description

Microorganisms and their balance in the human body are necessary to maintain the health. Once this equilibrium is broken, the excessive growth of pathogens occurs. Several factors lead to this imbalance, one of them is the mutation in the Transmembrane Conductivity Regulator gene of Cystic Fibrosis
