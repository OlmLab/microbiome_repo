Archive description

This study examined using whole genome shotgun sequencing, the oral microbiome collected from saliva as well as the gut microbiome collected from stool of subjects with well-controlled Crohn's disease (CD). The CD subjects were recruited from a mixed Asian population consisting of Han Chinese, Malay and Indian descent. Healthy controls who were age, race and gender matched were compared to the CD subjects.
