Archive description

Investigate the microecological distribution and compositional differences in the gut microbiota of individuals experiencing pregnancy loss compared to healthy controls using metagenomic sequencing.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: pregnant;healthy_volunteers.
