Gut microbiome and mental distress in pregnancy, Sweden & USA

BASIC: Biology, Affect, Stress, Imaging and Cognition in pregnancy

Shotgun metagenomics of 926 fecal samples from 530 pregnant women across 2nd and 3rd trimester in Swedish (n=765) and U.S. (n=161) cohorts, examining gut microbiome composition and gut-brain modules in relation to mental distress. machine-written summary
