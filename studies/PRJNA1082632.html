Archive description

Our aim was to characterize the gut microbiome of Indians residing in India, Indian immigrants in Canada and Indians born in Canada (Indo-Canadians) to assess the role of immigration and westernization on the gut microbiome in Indian populations.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.75. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: gut microbiome and immigration/westernization in Indian populations · population flags: healthy_volunteers.
