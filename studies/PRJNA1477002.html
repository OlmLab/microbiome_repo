Archive description

Fecal samples were collected from 20 college sprint athletes and 23 non athletic college students for metagenomic sequencing.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and adolescent by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.75. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: college athletes vs non-athletic students, gut microbiome · population flags: healthy_volunteers. Centre Anhui Medical University, first public 2026-06-16. Curated source gut_all_v1.
