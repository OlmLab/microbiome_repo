Archive description

This dataset comprises metagenomes from fecal samples, which were collected from elderly participants in the USA and Australia as part of the ASPREE-XT study, which is described in detail in PMC10330669

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and elderly by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
