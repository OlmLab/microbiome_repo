Archive description

Intestinal microbiome disruption is a risk factor for poor outcomes after allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplantation (allo-HCT), but the factors that contribute to microbiome injury are not well understood. We hypothesized that nutrition contributes to microbiome composition during allo-HCT.
