Longitudinal saliva bacteriome and early childhood caries

Longitudinal case-control study with 60 saliva specimens from infants and children (2 months-5 years) in Appalachia, USA, collected 2015-2017, analyzed by shotgun metagenomic DNA sequencing to study early childhood caries. machine-written summary

60 samples · 60 runs · US · Child · 2022 · 2.43 Gbp / sample

Library preparation
Illumina Nextera XT library preparation kit (shotgun); barcoded dual-index primers for 16S V4 amplicon
Sequencing
Illumina HiSeq (shotgun metagenomics); Illumina MiSeq (16S rRNA amplicon)
