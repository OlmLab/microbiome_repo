Archive description

This is a pilot study whose aim is to assess the feasibility of strain-level tracking of microbiome members shared by mothers and their children.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler and child and adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: mother_infant_pair. Centre University of Trento, first public 2016-10-01.
