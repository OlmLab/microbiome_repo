Archive description

This data was generated by shotgun sequencing for fecal samples from 10 patients after cerebral intraparenchymal hemorrhage and 10 feature-corresponding healthy controls.

How this project entered the catalog

The registry (archive-only classification) lists it as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and elderly. In the catalog: 20 DNA and 0 RNA shotgun sequencing runs (amplicon and single-cell runs are left out run by run).
