Archive description

This data was generated by shotgun sequencing for fecal samples from 10 patients after cerebral intraparenchymal hemorrhage and 10 feature-corresponding healthy controls.

How this project entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and elderly by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.80. Assay shotgun_dna.
