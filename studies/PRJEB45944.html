Archive description

Fecal metagenomes were sequenced from individuals with different long term diet habits. The aim of the project is to identify if there are any differences in gut metagenome associated with different long term diet habits and gut resistome.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.75. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: long term diet habits cohort · population flags: healthy_volunteers.
