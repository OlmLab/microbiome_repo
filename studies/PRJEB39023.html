Details
Archive description

In order to improve the understanding of microbial dynamics during Fecal Microbiota Transplantation, engraftment and persistence rates of donor and patient bacteria were studied at the strain-level using whole-genome shotgun metagenomic sequencing data from stool donors, recipients and post-FMT patients.
