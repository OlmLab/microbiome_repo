Details
Archive description

In this study, we have recruited volunteers (ten females and ten males, BMI 18.98-21.54) who had the allergy history or not.They were asked to take probiotics tablets (1010 CFU/day) for 28 days, and we collected their feces at baseline and at 28 days for metagenomic sequencing.

How this project entered the registry
