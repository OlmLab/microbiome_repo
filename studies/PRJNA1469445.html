Archive description

This project stores WGS metagenomics data of stool samples of 109 patients and control group participants from 3 hospitals of Central Region of Russia to study the impact of different therapy medications on gut microbiome composition. Faecal metagenomic samples were collected at two timepoints (before admission and after hospital discharge (median time of hospitalization 6 days)), sequenced and analyzed by ARGs and taxonomic composition.
