Archive description

Metagenomic sequencing was performed for paired human milk and infant fecal samples from the MILK study. Milk sequencing was performed at 1 month postpartum (and 3 months for a subset of samples), and infant fecal sequencing was performed at 1 and 6 months.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool / human milk shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic. Centre University of Minnesota, first public 2024-01-10.
