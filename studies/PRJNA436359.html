Details
Archive description

This project collected and profiled stool samples and rectal biopsies from pediatric patients with new-onset ulcerative colitis (UC) to evaluate the role of the gut microbiome in the efficacy of two conventional treatments.

How this project entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool / blood / tissue / internal fluids shotgun DNA metagenome of child by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Assay shotgun_dna.
