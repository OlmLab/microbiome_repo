Archive description

The data is about the fecal microbiome of patients with liver cancer and receiving immunotherapy. The correspondence between the sample labels in the NCBI BioProject and the sample labels in the manuscript is 10002 for R1, 10006 for R2, 10009 for R3, 10003 for NR1, 10007 for NR2, 10008 for NR3, 10010 for NR4 and 10011 for NR5.
