Details
Archive description

The study was aimed at characterizing off-target effects of albendazole, ivermectin-albendazole and moxidectin-albendazole on the bacterial gut microbiome via stool samples collected in a randomized-controlled phase 3 trial conducted in Cote d Ivoire in 2021. ClinicalTrials.gov: NCT04726969.
