Fiber-enriched bread & gut microbiota, cardiometabolic risk

Increasing the diversity of dietary fibres in a daily-consumed bread modifies gut microbiota and metabolic profile in subjects at cardiometabolic risk

156 stool samples from adults (and a few elderly) in France with cardiometabolic risk, studied in a double-blind, randomized, cross-over trial comparing standard bread versus a 7-dietary-fiber-enriched bread over two 8-week periods. machine-written summary
