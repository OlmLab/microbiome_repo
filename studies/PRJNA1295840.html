Archive description

Idiopathic nephrotic syndrome (INS) is the most frequent glomerular disorder in children, yet its etiology remains unclear. Growing evidence implicates gut microbiome dysbiosis and immune dysregulation in its pathogenesis, but comprehensive multi-kingdom microbiome studies are lacking.
