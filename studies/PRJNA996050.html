Archive description

In this study the fecal content of normal vermiform appendix was collected from n=14 adult pancreatic adenocarcinoma patients and age matched n=13 colon adenocarcinoma patients for metagenomic analysis, to compare microbiome profiles between the two patient groups.

How this study entered the registry
