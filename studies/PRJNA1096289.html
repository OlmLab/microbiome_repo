Archive description

Shotgun metagenomics sequencing of the fecal samples of 10-year-old Malian children collected before the transmission season. Used to determine if there is a difference between the baseline microbiome of children that remain asymptomatic and those that develop febrile symptoms after Plasmodium infection during the following malaria transmission season.

How this study entered the registry
