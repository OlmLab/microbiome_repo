Archive description

In order to evaluate the effects of gut microbiota composition on prostate cancer progression, we performed whole genome metagenome sequencing on gut microbiota of hormone sensitive prostate cancer vs castration resistant prostate cancer patients.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and elderly by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
