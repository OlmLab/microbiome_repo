Details
Archive description

This project contains raw metagenomic sequencing reads generated from maternal and infant gut microbiome samples. The study investigates gut microbial taxonomic and functional profiles in relation to maternal and infant metadata, including stress, sleep, physical activity, and feeding-related variables.

How this project entered the registry
