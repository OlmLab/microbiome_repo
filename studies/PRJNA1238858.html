Archive description

We conducted a randomized feeding trial to investigate the effects of intake timing and type of USFA on glycemic control in individuals with prediabetes.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.65. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: Prediabetes glycemic control feeding trial · population flags: disease_cohort. Interventions

This study administered: Controlled / prescribed diet or feeding regimen Design: randomized_controlled_trial. USFA (MUFA/PUFA) feeding timed at lunch vs dinner, 12-week RCT Trial population: type2_diabetes.

Evidence (abstract:40328731, route R4, confidence 0.5): “randomized, controlled, 2 × 2 factorial-designed feeding trial”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
