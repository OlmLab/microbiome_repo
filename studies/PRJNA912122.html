Gut microbiome in liver disease patients, USA

Lactulose-mediated Bifidobacteria expansion promotes a beneficial fecal metabolite profile that is associated with exclusion of antibiotic-resistant pathogens and improved outcomes in advanced liver disease

Shotgun metagenomics with paired faecal metabolite measurements of 850 stool samples from about 260 hospitalised patients with acute or chronic liver disease in Chicago, USA, studying lactulose, Bifidobacteria and infection outcomes. machine-written summary
