Archive description

Plasmids in the human microbiome are overlooked and should be integrated into investigations regarding their role in promoting health or disease. In order to investigate the human gut plasmidome, a database of human-associated metagenome assembled plasmid (MAP) database was constructed using whole genome sequencing data.
