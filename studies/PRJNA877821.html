Archive description

Metagenomic shotgun sequencing of fecal samples collected from patients with acute leukemia that were at some point colonized with multidrug-resistant Enterobacteriaceae

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.78.
