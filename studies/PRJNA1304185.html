Archive description

This public database provides longitudinal multi-omics data on 695 stool collected over 12 months from 280 Long COVID patients enrolled in a randomized clinical trial. Participants received either the probiotic formula SIM01 (specific Bifidobacterium strains with prebiotics) or placebo for 6 months, alongside clinical symptom tracking.
