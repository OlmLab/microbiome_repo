Archive description

Study analysed metagenomes derived from fecal samples from 28 individuals with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and 29 healthy controls. Paired metabolomic data was also generated for the same samples.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
