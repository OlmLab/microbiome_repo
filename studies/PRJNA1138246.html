Archive description

Fecal metagenomes from 54 healthy children living in peri-urban Lima, sampled longitudinally from 3-16 months. Children participated in an cohort study of enteric infections from 2016-2019 (NIH R01AI108695-01A )

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of child and infant / toddler by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
