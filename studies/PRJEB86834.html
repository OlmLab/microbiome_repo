Hi-C metagenomic reconstruction of bacterial and fungal microbiome colonizing gut-lung axis in severe-ill patients

Cross-sectional study of 11 gut (stool) samples from critically ill COVID-19 patients in Russia, with paired lung microbial isolates, examining Hi-C-resolved plasmid-mediated transmission of antimicrobial and antifungal resistance genes between gut and lung microbiomes. machine-written summary

Details
Archive description

In this project gut and sputum samples from severe-ill patients with confirmed COVID-19 infection diagnosis were used. These samples were WGS and Hi-C sequenced, microbiome and mycobiome were analyzed extensively.NOTE: samples SDD1,SDD2,SDD3,SDD4 and their respective files were renamed as COV8,COV9,COV10,COV11 to remove ambiguity in samples naming specification.

How this project entered the registry
