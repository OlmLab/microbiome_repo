Gut microbiota after FMT for GvHD in a Hong Kong teen

Fecal microbiota transplantations reconstitute gut fungal and viral microbiota in graft-versus-host disease

55 longitudinal fecal samples from one adolescent with graft-versus-host disease in Shatin, Hong Kong, tracking gut bacterial, fungal and viral communities before and after four weekly fecal microbiota transplantations. machine-written summary
