Archive description

we investigate longitudinal dynamics of gut bacterial, fungal and viral microbiota in a teenager with gut GvHD treated with multiple fecal microbiota transplantation by using 16s rRNA and metagenomics analysis

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: single_arm_before_after. Four weekly doses of FMT for gut GvHD in a teenager Trial population: transplant_or_immunocompromised.

Evidence (abstract:33397897, route R4, confidence 0.5): “after receiving four doses of FMT”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
