Details
Archive description

This dataset includes metagenomic sequencing data from neonates to investigate gut microbiome alterations in the context of immune modulation. The data represents three distinct groups: healthy controls, neonates with anti-Ro antibodies but without neonatal lupus erythematosus (NLE), and neonates diagnosed with NLE. This resource aims to provide insights into the potential role of gut microbiome dysbiosis in the development of autoimmune conditions during early life and its influence on immune system development.
