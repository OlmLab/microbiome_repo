PRJNA547591 SRP201343 DNA

Cameroon gut microbiome, GI infection vs healthy

Cameroon, U.S. fecal V4 amplicon sequencing of 16S rRNA and shotgun sequencing

Cross-sectional study of 180 fecal samples from Cameroon, profiled by 16S rRNA V4 amplicon and shotgun metagenomic sequencing, comparing samples with GI infection or diarrhoea (89) to healthy controls (91) across adult, child, adolescent, and elderly age groups. machine-written summary
