Archive description

Stool samples from a cohort of individuals in Gimbichu (Ethiopia, Oromia Region). Samples were sequenced with Illumina HiSeq 2500 using NexteraXT library preparation.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
