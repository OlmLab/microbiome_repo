Archive description

This study includes 208 samples from 45 infants over five time points including week1, week4, week8, week 24 and year2. We analysed the effect of antibiotic use in early life and mode of delivery on resistome and microbial profiles.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: antibiotic_trial. Centre Teagasc, first public 2023-06-05. This study administered: Antibiotic given as the study intervention (incl. eradication therapy Design: observational_with_procedure. Benzylpenicillin and gentamicin exposure in first 4 days of life
