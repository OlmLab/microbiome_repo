Archive description

Eight germ-free mice were inoculated with a single human stool sample. Eight weeks after inoculation, mice were sacrificed and luminal contents were collected from the duodenum, jejunum, ileum, cecum, and colon of each mouse and shotgun sequenced along with the human stool sample (the inoculum).

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human and non-human (mixed) gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.68. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5. Centre UCLA, first public 2026-02-22. Curated source gut_all_v1.
