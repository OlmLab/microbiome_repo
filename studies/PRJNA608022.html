Details
Archive description

Shotgun sequencing of 0.8 micron filtered and nuclease-digested viral fraction from human feces. Both viral DNA and RNA (via cDNA) was sequenced on Illumina platforms. Human reads were removed using Kneaddata and Pathseqs.
