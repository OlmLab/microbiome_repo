Archive description

Shotgun sequencing of 0.8 micron filtered and nuclease-digested viral fraction from human feces. Both viral DNA and RNA (via cDNA) was sequenced on Illumina platforms. Human reads were removed using Kneaddata and Pathseqs.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.60. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5. Centre J. Craig Venter Institute, first public 2020-05-27. Curated source gut_all_v1.
