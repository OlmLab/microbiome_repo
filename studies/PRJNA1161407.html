Details
Archive description

This project involves sequencing fecal microbiome samples to explore the composition and dynamics of microbial communities in the gut. The sequencing reads are part of a study focused on investigating the plasmids within these communities, with an emphasis on horizontal gene transfer in complex environments.

How this project entered the registry
