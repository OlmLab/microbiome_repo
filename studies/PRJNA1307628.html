Archive description

Microbiome alterations are associated with advanced chronic liver disease (ACLD), but mechanisms remain unclear. We analyzed saliva and fecal samples from ACLD patients, healthy controls, and septic controls. ACLD patients were stratified by disease stage (cACLD, AD, ACLF)
