Archive description

Analysis of childrens stool samples collected during a cluster randomized controlled trial of automated water chlorination at shared taps in two low income urban communities in Bangladesh. Stool samples were frozen but not preserved in conservation medium following collection.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of child and infant / toddler by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.60. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: Children in water chlorination intervention trial, Bangladesh · population flags: other.
