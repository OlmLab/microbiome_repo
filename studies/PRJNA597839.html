Details
Archive description

Stool samples from 31 patients with obesity, 30 healthy controls and 14 subjects after sleeve gastrectomy were collected and performed metagenomic shotgun sequencing. The gut microbiome was analysed for investigating the relationship with visceral fate and sleeve gastrectomy.
