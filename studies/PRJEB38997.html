Details
Archive description

To find associations between the taxonomical and functional profile of the gut metagenome and the development of retinopathia centralis Serosa (RCS), stool samples from 19 RCS patients and 19 controls with no signs of RCS were sequenced.

How this project entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.85. Assay shotgun_dna.
