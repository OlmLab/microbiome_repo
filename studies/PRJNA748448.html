Details
Archive description

The skin is a habitat for microbes that commonly infect preterm infants. However, the use of sequencing for fine-scale study of the microbial communities of skin that develop in these infants has been limited by technical barriers. Our study's goals are to overcome these technical barriers to sequencing the microbiome on preterm infant skin, and to examine the microbiome of preterm infant skin over their first eight weeks of life in the NICU.
