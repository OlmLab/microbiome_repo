Details
Archive description

To investigate Norovirus (NoV) epidemiology there is a need for whole genome sequences. We performed Illumina shotgun metagenomic sequencing on 71 RT-qPCR NoV positive feces (Ct below 30) from the Netherlands NoV surveillance program between 2015-109. Sixty complete and 10 partial NoV genomes were generated, as well as off-target Adenovirus, Aichivirus, Coxsackievirus, Bocaparvovirus, human Parechovirus, Rotavirus A and Sapovirus. We hope these genomes help contextualize outbreak investigation and genetic epidemiology.
