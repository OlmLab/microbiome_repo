Details
Archive description

Here we focus on the viral portion of the virome to assess possible viral involvement in disease processes, reasoning that the possible influences of the virome might be more obvious in very early onset (VEO) IBD, which is defined as onset of IBD before 6 years of age.

How this project entered the catalog

The registry (archive-only classification) lists it as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown. In the catalog: 82 DNA and 82 RNA shotgun sequencing runs (amplicon and single-cell runs are left out run by run).
