Details
Archive description

Gut microbial profiles from 7 children with ulcerative colitis were characterized by 16S rRNA gene sequencing before and after fecal microbiota transplantation.

How this project entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of child by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Assay shotgun_dna.
