Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Bacteriophage therapy Design: non_randomized_controlled. Fecal VLP (phage) transplant from UC patients or healthy controls into HMA mice, +/- DSS colitis Trial population: ulcerative_colitis.

Evidence (abstract:35799219, route R4, confidence 0.5): “fecal virus-like particles (VLPs) isolated from UC patients and healthy controls”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
