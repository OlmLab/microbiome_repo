Archive description

Two diets, Korean and Western diet. Each 6 individuals, half of which loose high weight and half of which loose little weight. Fecal samples taken before and after diet (3 months).

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.65. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: weight loss on Korean vs Western diet · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre UNIVERSITY OF SYDNEY, first public 2015-12-31. Curated source gut_all_v1.
