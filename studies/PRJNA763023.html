Details
Archive description

Our study comprehensively characterizes the association between gut microbiome dysbiosis and CRC risks in young or elderly groups, and highlights the great potential of gut microbiota biomarkers as a promising non-invasive tool in accurate detection and distinction of CRC onset based on age.
