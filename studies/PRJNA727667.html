Archive description

This project aims to explore the landscape of microbiome (bacteria, fungi and archaea) in the patients with lung adenocarcinoma and to reveal the causal relationship between microbiome and tumorigenesis.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human lower respiratory / gut / stool / blood / tissue / internal fluids shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.72. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: lung adenocarcinoma patients · population flags: disease_cohort.
