Details
Archive description

We examined the dynamics of microbiome functionality in Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) by profiling metagenomic and metatranscriptomic sequencing of the human gut microbiome among 100 individuals sampled over a one year period. Here, we present the first results based on the functional analysis of 78 paired metagenomes/metatranscriptomes and 222 additional metagenomes.
