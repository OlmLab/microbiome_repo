Details
Archive description

he gut microbiome plays an important role in early immune development, and differences in microbial composition have been linked to allergic conditions such as eczema and food allergy in infancy. This study used shotgun metagenomic sequencing to examine the gut microbiome of children under three years of age with physician-confirmed allergic conditions compared with non-allergic controls. The results identified age-specific microbial and functional differences that became more apparent during mid-infancy and toddlerhood, supporting a role for the gut microbiome in the development of early allergic disease.
