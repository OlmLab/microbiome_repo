Interventions

This study administered: Controlled / prescribed diet or feeding regimen Design: crossover. Multifunctional cereal products (polyphenols, fibers, slow-digestible starch, omega-3) vs control cereal products, 2 months Trial population: cardiometabolic_other.

Evidence (abstract:39710576, route R4, confidence 0.5): “consumed MF cereal-products, enriched with polyphenols, fibers, slowly-digestible starch, omega-3 fatty acids”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
