Longitudinal cohortread from the archive description (design reading)

Fecal samples from primarily and chronically HIV-infected and non-infected subjects in Mozambique were collected at up to three timepoints and sequenced by 16S V3-V4 and shotgun metagenomics. machine-written, checked

In the data: 21 people, all with other infection; 21 sampled more than once (median 3, up to 3 samples per person), followed for a median of 7 months; sampled at: gut / stool.
