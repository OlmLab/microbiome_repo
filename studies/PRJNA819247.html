Details
Archive description

Several studies have shown that maternal diet during pregnancy is associated with infant gut microbiome. However, the effect of maternal diet during lactation on infant gut microbiome has not been studied before.</p><p>Aims: To determine whether a two-week low-fat and low-sugar maternal dietary intervention during lactation is associated with changes in the infant gut microbiome composition and function.
