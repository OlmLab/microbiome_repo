Archive description

The purpose of this study was to investigate the use of shared common samples (human fecal material) with spike-in organisms and internal DNA controls from NIST RM 8376 to assess DNA extraction bias across different methodologies. Samples were prepared and shipped to individual laboratories, where DNA extraction was performed and the DNA extract was returned for common library prep, sequencing, and metagenomic analysis.
