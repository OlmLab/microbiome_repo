PRJNA383868 SRP104798 DNA

Oral microbiome in twins with dental caries, Australia

Metagenomics of the Microbiome in Oral Health and Disease

Shotgun metagenomic sequencing of supragingival plaque from 88 oral specimens of child and infant twins in Australia, collected 2014-2015, to study host genetic and environmental effects on caries. machine-written summary

88 samples · 88 runs · AU · Child · 2018 · 1.69 Gbp / sample

DNA / RNA extraction
lysozyme digest, bead beating, phenol/chloroform isoamyl alcohol extraction, ethanol precipitation
Library preparation
V4 16S rRNA PCR amplicon with dual index V2 chemistry (Kozich et al., 2013)
Sequencing
Illumina MiSeq
