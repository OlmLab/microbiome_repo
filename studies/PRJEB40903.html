Archive description

Our DNA extraction protocol is years old, and we want to compare new extraction methods to address outstanding issues. For more details, check out the motivation and experimental design here: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1qbuySJLEpxQjql-P6kJOk8QOS7Uo3MS1CL3QT56sDXI/edit?usp=sharing
