Archive description

This study investigates the human gut microbiome by employing both shotgun metagenomic and Hi-C sequencing techniques. Fecal samples from donors and recipients, who treated by LFMT or LSFF approaches, were collected before and after fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) to analyze microbial community composition and functionality.
