Archive description

This study was aimed at investigating the extent to which gut microbiome of patients with Anorexia Nervosa shifted upon fecal microbiome transplantation received from healthy donors. Microbiome taxonomical alterations as well as potential changes in functional profile of the gut microbome of patients were investigated

How this study entered the registry

Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: single_arm_before_after. Single FMT from healthy donors, oral capsules or rectal enema, in AN Trial population: other_disease.

Evidence (abstract:41535289, route R4, confidence 0.55): “FMT was administered either orally via capsules or as rectal enema”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
