Details
Archive description

The project involves an integrative multi-omics analysis of 30 Crohn’s disease patients after autologous hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT), with stool samples longitudinally collected over 5 years. The metagenome data here represents the gut microbiome from the selected samples from the cohort and ther corresponding humanised mouse models.
