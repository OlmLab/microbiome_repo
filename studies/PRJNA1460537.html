Archive description

Systemic sclerosis (SSc) is a complex autoimmune disorder that has been associated with gut microbial dysbiosis. In this study, we analyzed the characteristics of the gut microbiome composition and function in SSc patients and explored associations between microbial features and clinical phenotypes.
