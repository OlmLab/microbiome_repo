Archive description

Total microbial community DNA was isolated from the fecal samples of Japanese patients who underwent colonoscopy at National Cancer Research Center. The study population contains subjects diagnosed with Lynch Syndrome.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.65. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: Lynch Syndrome patients, colonoscopy cohort · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Tokyo Institute of Technology, first public 2024-06-30. Curated source gut_all_v1.
