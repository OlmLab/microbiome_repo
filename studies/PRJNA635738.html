Archive description

Breast milk (BM) harbors a diverse community of bacteria (microbiome) and viruses (virome) that are transmitted from mother-to-infant during breastfeeding and are important for establishing a healthy infant gut flora. Whether the breast milk microbiome and virome of women living with HIV are altered
