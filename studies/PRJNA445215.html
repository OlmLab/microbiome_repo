Archive description

Dental calculus (calcified dental plaque) is prevalent in archaeological skeletal collections, and a rich source of oral microbiome and host-derived ancient biomolecules. Recently, it has been proposed that dental calculus may provide a more robust environment for DNA preservation than other skeleta
