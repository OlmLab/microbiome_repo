Study design
Cross-sectional comparison of groupsread from the archive description (design reading)
groups sampled once and compared

Fecal metagenomes from Austrian senior men and women with and without metabolic syndrome were compared, three samples per sex/MS-status group (12 total). machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: male without MS (n = 3) · male with MS (n = 3) · female with MS (n = 3) · female without MS (n = 3)

In the data: 12 samples from older adults (which samples share a person is not recorded) in 3 groups (female with MS 3; male with MS 3; female without MS 3); sampled at: gut / stool.
