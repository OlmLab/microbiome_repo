Details
Archive description

Mucus samples from pediatric IBD patients and control non-IBD patients were collected from the appendix, peri-appendicular region, cecum, and ascending colon using sterile swabs immediately after excision. The samples were promptly frozen at -80C for subsequent DNA extraction and whole genome sequencing. Appendix tissues preserved in RNA-later were kept at room temperature for 1 hour before being frozen at -80C for DNA and RNA extraction, intended for 16S rRNA and RNA sequencing (GSE281635) respectively.
