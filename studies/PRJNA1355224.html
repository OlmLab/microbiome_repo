Archive description

The aim of the study was to define the bacterial composition, antibiotic resistance genes, and predicted metabolic pathways that are associated with risk of relapse to acute malnutrition or with differences between children discharged recovered or with moderate acute malnutrition post treatment.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler and child by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Washginton University in St. Louis, first public 2026-04-18.
