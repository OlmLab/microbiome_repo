Archive description

This study preliminarily explored the correlation between gut microbiota structure and function and the pharmacokinetics of oral tacrolimus in liver transplant recipients.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by opus_adjudicated with confidence 0.75. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-opus-5-5 · health context: Liver transplant recipients on oral tacrolimus · population flags: disease_cohort. Interventions

This study administered: Surgical procedure studied before/after (bariatric surgery Design: observational_with_procedure. Liver transplant recipients, tacrolimus dosage groups observed (not assigned) Trial population: transplant_or_immunocompromised.

Evidence (abstract:41059058, route R4, confidence 0.55): “fecal microbiota from 38 postliver transplant patients and 31 healthy controls”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
