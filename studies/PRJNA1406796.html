Archive description

Prolonged acute gastroenteritis in infants often poses a diagnostic challenge, as standard cultures and targeted panels may fail to identify unconventional or mixed pathogens. Clostridium perfringens is typically associated with foodborne illness mediated by enterotoxins, but its role in prolonged pediatric diarrhea, especially alongside viral agents, is poorly defined.
