The study recruited 48 healthy Caucasian males born at full term (1979–1980) to test whether 12 weeks of exercise training reverts cardiometabolic effects of 4 weeks of carbohydrate overfeeding; gut metagenomes were sequenced from fecal samples. machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: Overfeeding (OF) (n = 48) · Exercise training (after overfeeding, BL/other time point) (n = 48)

In the data: 48 adults in 3 arms (Control / pre-intervention baseline 47; Controlled / prescribed diet or feeding regimen 42; Exercise / physical-training programme 41); 43 sampled more than once (median 3, up to 3 samples per person), followed for a median of 4 months; sampled at: gut / stool.

Details
Archive description

This study aimed to investigate whether 12 weeks of exercise training can revert and/or minimize the deleterious cardiometabolic effects of 4 weeks of carbohydrate overfeeding in individuals born with low birth weight and increased risk of developing type 2 diabetes. This study recruited a total of 48 healthy Caucasian males, born between 1979 and 1980 at full term (gestational weeks 39–41)
