Archive description

Macrogene sequencing technology was used to sequence and analyze the collected neonatal feces with jaundice.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler and neonate by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic. Centre Shanghai Ocean University, first public 2026-04-19. Interventions

This study administered: Probiotic (live microorganisms; single strain or mixture) Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Bifidobacterium M-16V and/or Bb-12 probiotics vs phototherapy-only control, 4 arms Trial population: other_disease.

Evidence (abstract:41994268, route R4, confidence 0.5): “Phototherapy Control, M-16V, Bb-12, and the combined M-16V+Bb-12 group”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
