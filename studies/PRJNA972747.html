Archive description

The role of bile acids in acute graft-host-disease (GVHD) after allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplantation (allo-HCT). This study is performed on stool samples from patients after allo-HCT at time of GVHD onset and matched controls as well as cecal content from mice after transplant of bone marrow only or bone marrow + T cells.

How this study entered the registry
