Archive description

We used untargeted metagenomic Illumina sequencing to study fecal samples of 35 Swedish healthcare students, taken before and after embarking on a student exchange program to the Indian peninsula or central Africa. The students were not taking antibiotics between sampling points. Antibiotic resistance gene frequencies were investigated in all samples.
