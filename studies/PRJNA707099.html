Body fluid metagenomics for occult malignancy detection, USA

Cryptogenic Malignancies in Body Fluids

Shotgun metagenomic sequencing (human reads subtracted) of 112 body fluid specimens, mainly blood/tissue and lower respiratory fluid, from patients screened for malignancy or infection via CNV detection. machine-written summary
