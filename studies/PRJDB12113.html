Details
Archive description

Metagenomic next-generation sequencing for detecting causativepathogens in the blood and cerebrospinal fluid with pediatricmeningitis. The data is included FastQ from cerebrospinal fluid and blood with meningitis patients. The human genome sequences were excluded from all FastQ.
