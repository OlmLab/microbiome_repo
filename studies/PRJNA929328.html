Archive description

ONT WG sequencing of stool samples in alloHSCT patients taken at multiple timepoints throughout therapy as well as additional samples containing Zymo Gut Microbiome Std. and a control group of 10 individuals.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
