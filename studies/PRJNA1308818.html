Archive description

Metagenomic cohort of individuals living in the same household, with availability of paired oral and fecal samples. This is used to evaluate oral and gut microbiome transmission in the context of households as well as oral-gut transmission.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool / oral cavity shotgun DNA metagenome of child and adult and adolescent by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.57. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: household cohabitant microbiome transmission · population flags: mother_infant_pair.
