Archive description

Longitudinal metagenomic sequencing data for a Randomized-Controlled Trial of Fecal Microbiota Transplantation in ulcerative colitis. For a detailed sample description, see Rosen et al, 2015 (PMID:25836986).

Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: randomized_controlled_trial. FMT via nasoduodenal tube vs autologous (control) in UC, double-blind RCT Trial population: ulcerative_colitis.

Evidence (abstract:25836986, route R4, confidence 0.55): “underwent FMT with feces from healthy donors or were given autologous fecal”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
