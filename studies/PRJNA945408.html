Study design
Non-randomised intervention studyread from the archive description (design reading)
arms assigned without randomisation

Fecal samples were collected longitudinally from participants in two military-led clinical trials: TrEAT TD, evaluating three single-dose antibiotic regimens to treat travelers' diarrhea, and PREVENT TD, evaluating rifaximin prophylaxis during international deployment. Samples came from Kenya and Honduras. machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: TrEAT TD (treatment trial) · PREVENT TD (prophylaxis trial)

In the data: 163 adults, all with gi infection / diarrhoea; 163 sampled more than once (median 2, up to 6 samples per person), followed for a median of 35 days; sampled at: gut / stool.

Details
Archive description

We applied shotgun metagenomic and whole genome sequencing to a collection of longitu-dinally-collected fecal samples and cultured bacteria collected during two military-led clinical trials: TrEAT TD , which evaluated the effectiveness of three single-dose antibiotic reg-imens to treat TD, and PREVENT TD, which evaluated the effectiveness of the antibiotic rifaximin as a prophylaxis to prevent TD during international deployment.
