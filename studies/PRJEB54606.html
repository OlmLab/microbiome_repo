Archive description

To date, it is unknown why the pediatric onset UC-phenotype appears to be more aggressive than that seen in adult-onset UC. To evaluate microbial differences between the two groups both longitudinal fecal samples and multi-site biopsies were collected from the patients. Fecal samples were subjected to shotgun metagenomic sequencing and biopsies to amplicon 16S rRNA gene sequencing.
