Archive description

Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Defined bacterial consortium / live biotherapeutic product Design: randomized_controlled_trial. 15-strain LBP (MTC01) vs same-donor FMT, low/high dose Trial population: gi_infection_or_diarrhoea.
