Details
Archive description

This study investigates the effects of fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) on gut microbiota composition and weight loss in overweight and obese individuals. Using metagenomic sequencing, we analyzed microbial profiles of both donors and recipients before and after FMT to evaluate the role of donor-derived strains in promoting weight loss.
