Details
Archive description

Bacterial communities of patient fecal samples were profiled via V3-V4 region 16S rRNA gene sequencing at four time points: i) prior to both fecal microbiota transplant (FMT) and anti-PD-1 immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI) therapy, ii) post-FMT (7 days) / pre-ICI, iii) post-FMT / post-ICI (3 weeks), and iv) post-FMT / post-ICI (3 months)
