Details
Archive description

Oral, vaginal, rectal and fecal samples from 160 healthy young women under different contraceptive regimens over the course of a full menstrual cycle. * Rectal swabs: one per woman, in the menstrual phase * Saliva samples: 3 per woman (menstrual, follicular, luteal) * Fecal samples: 3 per woman (menstrual, follicular, luteal) * Vaginal samples: 3 per woman (menstrual, follicular, luteal) * Vaginal samples: 39 amplicon samples over a 6 week period, 15 women * Vaginal samples: 28 shotgun samples over a 4 week period, 49 women
