Archive description

Sequences were produced by amplicon sequencing of V3-V4 region of the 16S gene and shotgun metagenomic sequencing from stool samples of 9 healthy individuals in Maribor, Slovenia, sampled for 6 months approximately 14 days. Goal of the study was to observe and describe the longitudinal persistence and stability of ethanol resistant spore-forming bacteria in the healthy human gut.
