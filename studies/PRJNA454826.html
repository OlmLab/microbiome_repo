Archive description

In this clinical study, we investigated the effect of high-intensity exercise intervention on glycaemic control in human and performed shotgun metagenomic sequencing to elucidate the role of human gut microbiota within such process.

How this study entered the registry

Interventions

This study administered: Exercise / physical-training programme Design: single_arm_before_after. 12-week high-intensity exercise training in prediabetic males Trial population: type2_diabetes.
