Archive description

Faecal Microbiota Transplantation (FMT) effectively induces remission in ulcerative colitis (UC). In the randomised, controlled FOCUS Study of FMT in active UC we aimed to identify the bacterial taxonomic and functional determinants of therapeutic outcome.
