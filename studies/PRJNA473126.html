Archive description

This is a short-read-sequence based longitudinal analysis of gut microbiome development in healthy twins 0-8 months of age in St Louis, Missouri.

The registry (archive-only classification) lists it as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler and adult from the archive labels (deterministic rule, carried from an earlier release) with confidence 0.90.
