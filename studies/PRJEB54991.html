Archive description

Study characterising the effect of the supplementation of infant formula with the Human Milk Oligosaccharide (HMO) 2-fucosyllactose (2FL) on the gut microbiome. Breastfed and formula fed infants were included. Formula fed infants received either a formula with or without 2FL.
