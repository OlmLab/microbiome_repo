A randomized controlled trial comparing HIV-exposed, uninfected South African infants given prophylactic cotrimoxazole versus those not given it, with gut microbiome sampling over infant development. machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: Cotrimoxazole · Control

In the data: 64 infants in 2 arms (Antibiotic given as the study intervention 34; Control / pre-intervention baseline 30); 61 sampled more than once (median 3, up to 4 samples per person), followed for a median of 4 months; sampled at: gut / stool.
