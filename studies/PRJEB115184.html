Archive description

This clinical trial investigated whether combining Fecal Microbiota Transplantation (FMT) with the Crohn's Disease Exclusion Diet (CDED) is more effective than CDED alone (sham + CDED) for treating mild-to-moderate Crohn's disease. The FMT + CDED combination showed significant benefits: it dramatically reduced long-term treatment failure (5.9% vs 46.2%), improved combined clinical and endoscopic responses, and successfully promoted the lasting engraftment of beneficial gut bacteria.
