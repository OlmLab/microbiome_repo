Intestinal Virome Changes Precede Autoimmunity in Type I Diabetes Susceptible Children

Cohort-longitudinal study of 220 gut virome samples from children in Finland (Jorvi) and Estonia (Tartu), comparing 163 healthy-control samples with 57 type 1 diabetes samples to examine virome changes preceding autoimmunity. machine-written summary
