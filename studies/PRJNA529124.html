Archive description

Stool samples of a cohort of individuals from Ghana (Asante Akim North district). Samples were sequenced with Illumina HiSeq 2500 using NexteraXT library preparation.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic. Centre Polo Scientifico e Tecnologico Fabio Ferrari, first public 2019-03-28.
