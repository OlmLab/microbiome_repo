Archive description

Shotgun metagenomic sequencing of DNA from fecal samples of patients with ulcerative colitis being treated with oral lyophilized fecal microbiota transplantation. Patients were recruited as part of the LOTUS study.

How this study entered the registry

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Oral lyophilized donor FMT vs placebo in ulcerative colitis patients Trial population: ulcerative_colitis.
