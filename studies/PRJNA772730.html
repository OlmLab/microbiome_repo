Archive description

The proposed clinical study will evaluate the tolerability of feeding preterm infants a B. infantis probiotic (EVC001) as the primary endpoint. This particular strain of Bifidobacterium has been shown to uniquely utilize oligosaccharides found in human breast milk, possibly providing a nutritional benefit for the infant consuming breast milk when B. infantis is colonized in the gut.

Interventions

This study administered: Probiotic (live microorganisms; single strain or mixture) Design: non_randomized_controlled. B. infantis EVC001 once-daily feedings, 8.0x10^9 CFU in MCT oil Trial population: preterm_nicu.

Evidence (abstract:35071138, route R4, confidence 0.55): “once-daily feedings of B. infantis EVC001”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
