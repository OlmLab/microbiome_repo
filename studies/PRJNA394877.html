Archive description

High frequency intestinal microbiota sampling in allogeneic bone marrow transplant patients, part of a larger systems biology project examining factors that influence antibiotic resistance, with a focus on the pathogens Vancomycin-Resistant Enterococcus and Klebsiella pneumoniae
