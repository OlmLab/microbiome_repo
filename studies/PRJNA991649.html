Archive description

Feces were collected from Asian women before receiving frozen embryo transfer with OMNIgeneGUT kits. DNA was isolated and subjected to shotgun metagenomic sequencing.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: pregnant. Centre The University of Hong Kong, first public 2025-01-29.
