Archive description

Longitudinal metagenomic sequencing data for a Randomized-Controlled Trial of Fecal Microbiota Transplantation in patients with metabolic syndrome. Extension to a previous dataset (PRJEB12357), described by Li et al, 2016 (PMID:27126044) and Koote et al, 2017 (PMID:28978426).

Interventions

This study administered: Faecal microbiota transplantation Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Lean donor (allogenic) vs autologous FMT in metabolic syndrome patients Trial population: cardiometabolic_other.

Evidence (abstract:28978426, route R4, confidence 0.55): “lean donor (allogenic) versus own (autologous) fecal microbiota transplantation”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
