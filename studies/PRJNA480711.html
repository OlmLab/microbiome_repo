Details
Archive description

Using a multi-omics approach, we identified microbiome-derived</p><p>biochemicals and metagenomic functions associated with progressive hepatic pathology and fibrosis in Latino children (N=241). These findings point to potential therapeutic targets in NASH and hepatic injury.
