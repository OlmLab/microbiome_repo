Archive description

Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is a public health threat where efficient</p><p>surveillance is needed to prevent outbreaks. Existing methods for detection of</p><p>gastrointestinal colonization of multidrug-resistant organisms (MDRO) are limited to</p><p>specific organisms or resistance mechanisms. Metagenomic next-generation</p><p>sequencing (mNGS) is a more rapid and agnostic diagnostic approach for microbiome</p><p>and resistome investigations. We determined if mNGS can detect MDRO from rectal</p><p>swabs in concordance with standard microbiology results. Methods: We performed and</p><p>compared mNGS performance on short-read Illumina MiSeq (N=10) and long-read</p><p>Nanopore MinION (N=4) platforms directly from peri-rectal swabs to detect</p><p>vancomycin-resistant enterococci (VRE) and carbapenem-resistant Gram-negative</p><p>organisms (CRO).
