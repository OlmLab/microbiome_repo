Details
Archive description

Virome and metagenome sequencing of the human mucosal-luminal interface from five pediatric subjects with Crohn's disease. Samples were obtained from the proximal and distal colon during colonoscopy and subjected to 1) virus-like particle enrichment and sequencing 2) whole metagenome sequencing.
