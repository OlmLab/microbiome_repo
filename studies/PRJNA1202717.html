Archive description

This dataset supports a study investigating the effects of recent antibiotic use on gut microbiota recovery in stool donors from Canada and Finland. Using longitudinal stool sampling and metagenomic sequencing, the research explores the lasting disruptions antibiotics cause to bacterial diversity, community composition, microbial functions, viral populations, and antibiotic resistant genes in donors.
