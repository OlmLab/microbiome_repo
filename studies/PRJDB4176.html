Details
Archive description

Total microbial community DNA was isolated from the fecal samples of Japanese patients who underwent colonoscopy at National Cancer Research Center. The study population contains health, early and advanced cancer stages.

How this project entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Assay shotgun_dna.
