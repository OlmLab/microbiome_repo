Archive description

Gut microbiota metagenomic analysis of Neonatal Respiratory Distress Syndrome (NRDS) patients and healthy controls using whole-genome shotgun sequencing. REUSE CONDITIONS: These data are associated with a manuscript currently under review. Any reuse of this data must properly cite this BioProject (PRJNA1231247) and the eventual publication.

How this study entered the registry
