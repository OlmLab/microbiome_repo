Archive description

To investigate the role of intestinal mucosal microbiota in colon cancer, we colected tumor and adiacentnon-cancerous mucosal samples from colon cancer patients for Metagenomics sequencing to further elucidate the functional composition of colon cancer mucosal microbiota.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult and elderly by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.78.
