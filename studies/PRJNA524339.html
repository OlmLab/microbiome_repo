Archive description

Shotgun fecal samples from men who have sex with men HIV at risk and infected subjects were assessed for microbial taxa and metabolism association with depression measures.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by opus_adjudicated with confidence 0.70. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-opus-5-5 · health context: HIV-infected and at-risk MSM; depression · population flags: disease_cohort.
