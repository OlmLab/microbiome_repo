Microbiota & B12 genes in parasite-infected children

Impact of Intestinal Parasites on Microbiota and Cobalamin Gene Sequences

Cross-sectional study of 37 stool samples from children/infants in Argentina, comparing Giardia, helminth, and co-infection groups to examine effects on gut microbiota diversity and cobalamin (vitamin B12) synthesis genes. machine-written summary
