Details
Archive description

Gut microbial communities developed by in vitro incubations from fecal inoculums are important research tools. The extent to which the stable developed communities resemble the inoculums can vary with experimental conditions and across donors. Despite these variables, certain taxa persist in the inoculum, while other do not persist. These differences are common and can have important implications for experimental design.
