Archive description

This is a large metagenomic dataset of children with ASD and children that are neurotypical in Hong Kong. This dataset will continue to be updated as more metagenomic data are included.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of child by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90.
