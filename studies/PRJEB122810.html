Archive description

The effect of cobamide supplementation on microbiota composition, especially on R. gnavus growth, was studied in a batch fermentation model using a human faecal obtained from a healthy donor positive for the presence of R. gnavus (as determined by qPCR). Here, we tested a low dose (5 ng/mL) or high dose (2500 ng/mL) of cyanopseudocobalamin (CNPsCbl), and micriobiota composition was determined by shotgun metagenomics before fermentation (0h) and after 4h and 8h of fermentation.
