Archive description

Longitudinal crossover shotgun metagenomics study to investigate the impact of dietary supplementation with two types of Resistant Starches (RS) on the human gut microbiome composition and functional potential. The stool samples used to generate the shotgun metagenomics sequencing data are also used in PRJNA944997.
