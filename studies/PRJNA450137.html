Tracheal aspirate specimens from 92 critically ill adults with acute respiratory failure in the USA were profiled by combined metagenomic and metatranscriptomic sequencing to assess pathogens, airway microbiome and host transcriptome for LRTI diagnosis. machine-written summary

Details
Archive description

Metagenomic next-generation sequencing (mNGS) was performed on tracheal aspirates from adults with acute respiratory failure to simultaneously assess pathogens, the lung microbiome and the host transcriptome.
