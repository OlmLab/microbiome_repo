Details
Archive description

This BioProject contains shotgun metagenomic sequencing data generated from human fecal samples collected from adults aged 50 years and older enrolled in a randomized, placebo-controlled clinical trial investigating the effects of KM2-HPB01 administration on gut microbiome composition. Paired-end Illumina sequencing was performed to characterize microbial community structure and functional potential.
