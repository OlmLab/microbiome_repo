Details
Archive description

Endotracheal aspirate metagenomic sequences obtained during the ASPIRE-ICU study. The dataset contains the samples used for the cross-sectional analysis of patients developing pneumonia and paired controls, and for the longitudinal analysis performed in conjunction with samples from the SAATELLITE trial.
