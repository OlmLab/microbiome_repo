Archive description

Shotgun macrogenome sequence collected from fecal samples of 13 subjects after intervention with multiphase dietetic protocol incorporating an improved ketogenic diet at week 0 and 12.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic. Centre Jiangnan University, first public 2026-08-16. Curated source gut_all_v1.

Interventions

This study administered: Controlled / prescribed diet or feeding regimen Design: single_arm_before_after. Multiphase modified ketogenic diet (MDP-i-KD), 12 weeks Trial population: obesity.

Evidence (bioproject.description, route R4, confidence 0.55): “improved ketogenic diet at week 0 and 12”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
