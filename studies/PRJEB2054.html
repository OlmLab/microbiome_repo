Study design
Case–control studyread from the archive description (design reading)
people with a condition compared with people without it

124 individuals from Denmark and Spain had gut metagenomes sequenced by deep metagenomic sequencing, generating 577 Gb of data. machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: MH (healthy) · UC (ulcerative colitis) · CD (Crohn's disease)

In the data: 124 samples from adults (which samples share a person is not recorded) in 3 groups (MH 85; UC 27; CD 12); sampled at: gut / stool.

Each circle: samples in one group at one body site (area ∝ count; hover for people and samples). Rows come from comparison_group, columns from body_site_code.
Paper and data checks
  • !The paper states 124 participants, but no variable says which samples came from the same person, so the samples cannot be grouped by participant.
  • ✓Groups are told apart by each sample’s comparison_group (3 groups).
  • iBody site is the project’s single stated site; the samples themselves do not record it.
