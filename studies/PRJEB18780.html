PRJEB18780 ERP020739

Gut microbiota in Crohn's relatives vs healthy controls, children

Exploring associations between the gut microbiota and colonic inflammation and assessing sequential changes during exclusive enteral nutrition (EEN) for children with Crohn's disease and comparing them to healthy cohorts as well as their relatives

96 faecal samples from adult relatives of children with Crohn's disease, unrelated healthy adults, and children with Crohn's disease, comparing microbiota composition during exclusive enteral nutrition in a case-control design. machine-written summary
