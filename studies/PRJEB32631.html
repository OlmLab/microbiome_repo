Archive description

Study to investigate how early microbe exposure and the developing immune system influence subsequent health and developmental outcomes. This data is part of a pre-publication release. For information on the proper use of pre-publication data shared by the Wellcome Trust Sanger Institute (including details of any publication moratoria), please see http://www.sanger.ac.uk/datasharing/
