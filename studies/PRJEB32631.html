Sampling design and relatedness

longitudinalmother–infant

4 of 2,381 people sampled more than once (9 samples; median 2, up to 3 per person) · follow-up median 6 d (max 14 d) · median interval between samples 3 d (time from age at collection). Schedule stated by the source: “Neonatal samples at day 4, 7, and/or 21 (<1 month), with a subset re-sampled in infancy (8.75±1.98 months); mothers sampled once (matched).” (time origin: birth); stated follow-up 12 mo.
