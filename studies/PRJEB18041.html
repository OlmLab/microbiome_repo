Archive description

Stool samples were collected from the patients with alcohol dependence syndrome and alcoholic liver cirrhosis from Russian Federation, subject to total DNA extraction and whole-genome metagenomic sequencing using SOLiD 5500xl platform.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of adult by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.70. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: Alcohol dependence syndrome and alcoholic liver cirrhosis · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Federal Research Clinical Centre of Physical-Chemical Medicine, first public 2016-12-06. Curated source gut_all_v1.
