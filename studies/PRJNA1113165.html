Study design
Randomised controlled trialread from the paper (design reading)
people randomly assigned to arms

Mother-infant dyads in the MISAME-III trial in rural Burkina Faso were randomised to maternal balanced energy-protein (BEP) plus IFA supplementation or IFA-only control, with maternal stool at second/third trimester and 1-2 and 5-6 months postpartum, and infant stool at 1-2 and 5-6 months. machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: Intervention (BEP+IFA) mothers (n = 71) · Control (IFA only) mothers (n = 81) · Intervention (BEP+IFA) infants (n = 71) · Control (IFA only) infants (n = 81)

In the data: 298 people; 257 sampled more than once (median 3, up to 4 samples per person); sampled at: gut / stool.

Interventions

intervention — arms not resolved

Non-microbial dietary supplement (vitamins, minerals, omega-3, polyphenols, specific foods as supplement) design randomised controlled trial · population pregnant and lactating women and their infants in rural Burkina Faso · Balanced energy-protein (BEP) lipid-based nutrient supplement (72 g/day, 393 kcal) fortified with micronutrients during pregnancy and first 6 months of lactation; all women also received iron-folic acid (IFA) tablets and malaria prophylaxis (sulfadoxine-pyrimethamine)

“Balanced energy-protein (BEP) supplementation during pregnancy and lactation can improve birth outcomes and infant growth, with the gut microbiome as a potential mediator.” — paper.fulltext.abstract. This quote describes the whole study; each sample's arm is in the sample table at the bottom of the page.

Details
Archive description

we use deep metagenomic sequencing to characterise the composition and functionality of the gut microbiome in pregnant and lactating women and their infants within a randomised controlled trial conducted in rural Burkina Faso.
