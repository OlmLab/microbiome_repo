Archive description

Metagenomic sequencing samples from a randomized, controlled, crossover dietary trial to investigate the risk of transmitting antimicrobial resistance genes (ARG) to consumers through beef consumption.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human and non-human (mixed) gut / stool / other human site shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.50. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5 · health context: beef consumption, antimicrobial resistance gene transmission trial · population flags: disease_cohort. Centre Texas A&M University, first public 2026-02-07. Curated source gut_all_v1.
