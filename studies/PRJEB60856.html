Study design
Cross-sectional comparison of groupsread from the paper
groups sampled once and compared

Supragingival plaque samples from individual teeth of 54 adults were classified as caries-free (PF) or having an active dentin carious lesion (PD), and rpoC gene amplicon sequencing was used to characterize the microbiome composition of each sample. machine-written, checked

Groups the source compares: PF (caries-free) · PD (active dentin caries)

In the data: 60 samples from adults (which samples share a person is not recorded); sampled at: oral cavity.

Paper and data checks
  • !The paper states 54 participants, but no variable says which samples came from the same person, so the samples cannot be grouped by participant.
  • !The source compares groups, but no sample variable says which group each sample belongs to.
