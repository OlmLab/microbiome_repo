Archive description

Total microbial community DNA was isolated from the fecal samples of healthy adults who received prebiotics diet.

How this study entered the registry

Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model deterministic · population flags: healthy_volunteers. Centre Kasetsart University, first public 2024-06-04. Interventions

This study administered: Prebiotic / dietary fibre supplement Design: single_arm_before_after. Copra meal hydrolysate (CMH) prebiotic treatment in healthy Thai adults Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (bioproject.description, route R4, confidence 0.5): “healthy adults who received prebiotics diet”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
