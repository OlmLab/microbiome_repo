Archive description

The present study is intended to answer the question whether probiotic ingestion can alter gut microbiome composition and epithelial cells permeability, influence the inflammatory response, and thereby improve aerobic capacity.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.62. Interventions

This study administered: Non-microbial dietary supplement (vitamins Probiotic (live microorganisms; single strain or mixture) Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Probiotic + vitamin D3 vs vitamin D3, double-blind, 4 weeks Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (abstract:37885441, route R4, confidence 0.55): “randomly assigned to either the probiotic + vitamin D 3”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
