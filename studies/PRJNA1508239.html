Archive description

Shotgun metagenomic sequencing was performed on serial stool samples collected from children with acute lymphoblastic leukemia during the first six months of chemotherapy. Samples were obtained at diagnosis, end of induction, and approximately six months after diagnosis to characterize longitudinal changes in microbial taxonomic composition and functional potential. The dataset includes de-identified samples collected within a prospective dietary counseling study.
