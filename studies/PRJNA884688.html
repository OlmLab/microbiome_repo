Archive description

The goal of this study is to identify features of human infant gut microbiota that differ based on source of dietary protein

This study administered: Controlled / prescribed diet or feeding regimen Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Meat- vs dairy-based complementary foods, 5-12 months Trial population: healthy_control.
