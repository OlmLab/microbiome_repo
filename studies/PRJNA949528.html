Details
Archive description

We are studying the diversity and composition of the gut metagenome isolated from children immediately after birth and up to 12 months of age in Kazakhstan in order to deepen our understanding of the development of the microflora in early life and look for possible factors influencing the change in the developmental trajectory of the microbiome of children.
