Interventions

This study administered: Antibiotic given as the study intervention (incl. eradication therapy Design: randomized_controlled_trial. 372 µg/day ciprofloxacin vs placebo for 27 days Trial population: healthy_control.

Evidence (abstract:41057417, route R4, confidence 0.55): “randomized 30 individuals to either 372 µg of ciprofloxacin or placebo daily”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
