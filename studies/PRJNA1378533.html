Archive description

First 12week treatment period and considered only two timepoints: baseline (Visit 2, V2) and the end of the initial treatment period after 12 weeks of probiotic or placebo supplementation. Shotgun 2x151bp Illumina MiSeq sequences

Interventions

This study administered: Probiotic (live microorganisms; single strain or mixture) Design: randomized_controlled_trial. Multi-strain probiotic 12 weeks vs placebo, with metformin Trial population: type2_diabetes.

Evidence (bioproject.description, route R4, confidence 0.5): “12 weeks of probiotic or placebo supplementation”. Study-level: it says what the study gave, not which samples received it — per-sample arms appear as the intervention field when the archive records them.
