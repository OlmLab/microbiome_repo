Gut microbiome and exercise in collegiate male athletes, Poland

The impact of exercise-induced muscle damage, inflammation and oxidative stress on the intestinal microbiota in athletes.

Shotgun metagenomic sequencing of 232 stool samples from 52 adult men (endurance athletes, strength athletes and non-athletic controls) collected before and after Bruce treadmill and Wingate tests, Gdansk, Poland. machine-written summary
