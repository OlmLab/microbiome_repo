Details
Archive description

This exploratory study found associations between cervical neutrophil gene expression and relative abundance of cervicovaginal microbiota. These results suggest that cervical immune populations need to be further investigated, to explore their relationship with the cervicovaginal microbiome and their possible role in sPTB.
