Archive description

We performed a deep (average > 30Gbp) whole metagenomic shotgun sequencing on 106 Korean samples. Metagenomic de-novo assembly was performed on the samples.

How this study entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of age unknown by sonnet_x2 with confidence 0.75. Registry row: open the registry record · assay shotgun_dna · access open · model claude-sonnet-5. Centre Yonsei University, first public 2021-05-20. Curated source gut_all_v1.
