Archive description

These samples were generated to quantify and interrogate the antibiotic resistome within a cohort of premature infants, using shotgun metagenomic sequencing.

How this project entered the registry

Classified as human gut / stool shotgun DNA metagenome of infant / toddler and neonate by deterministic_prior with confidence 0.90. Assay shotgun_dna.
