Fields & vocabularies

What each field means, how often it has a value, and where those values came from.

About this page

The catalog curates 5 core fields (body_site_code, age_category, country, health_condition, subject_id) and 14 key fields (age_at_collection_days, sex, detailed_location, lifestyle, collection_date, intervention, antibiotic_exposure, bmi, timepoint_label, comparison_group, diet, smoking_status, medication, stool_consistency_bristol) for every sample — plus the 9 birth and feeding fields, which exist only where a project was curated in depth. Fields whose column is not yet in this package are omitted below. Every committed value carries the source it came from (archive record · supplementary table · paper text · abstract), what it applies to (one sample, or a statement about the whole cohort applied to a project’s samples), a confidence and a verbatim evidence quote. Per sample and field the archive record wins over the supplementary table, then the paper text, then the abstract; the in-depth curation wins for the projects it covers (Where values come from). Coverage below is the share of all 813,356 samples with a value.

Definitions and caveats

fieldvocabulary / valuesdefinition and caveats
body_site_codeWhere on the body the sample was taken (gut / stool, mouth, skin, nose, airway, urogenital, milk, blood / tissue, eye / ear, other, several sites). From the sample's own archive record or, when the project sampled a single site, the project's site from the scope classification of the archive description and abstract. 'unknown_site' (no site stated) counts as not covered.
age_categoryneonate, infant, child, adolescent, adult, elderlyage at collection at the level of a life stage: from the exact age when known (age_at_collection_days, bins above), else the sample's own life-stage attribute, else a cohort statement in the paper text or the abstract, else the study's single registry life stage; the basis is recorded per sample in age_category_basis. 'unknown' = not covered. Per-category cells for this row: share of the category whose life stage comes from an exact age (the rest rests on a sample life-stage attribute or a cohort statement).
countryISO-3166 alpha-2ISO-2 of the sampling / recruitment country from archive attributes, tables or paper text (not the laboratory or ethics-committee country).
health_conditionhealthy_control, ibd_unspecified, crohns_disease, ulcerative_colitis, ibs, colorectal_cancer, colorectal_adenoma, other_cancer, obesity, type2_diabetes, type1_diabetes, cardiometabolic_other, liver_disease, kidney_disease, gi_infection_or_diarrhoea, other_infection, autoimmune_inflammatory, neurological_psychiatric, allergy_or_atopy, malnutrition, transplant_or_immunocompromised, pregnancy_postpartum, intervention_cohort, other_disease, unknown, preterm_nicu, nec, sepsis_or_infection, ibd_or_gi_diseaseControlled vocabulary. Cohort-wide codes from the paper text or abstract are committed only when every sequenced person shares the condition; case–control cohorts stay unknown at sample level unless the table or attribute names the group.
subject_idstudy's own subject identifier (free text)Submitter identifier of the individual (host_subject_id, participant, donor …); never a sample id. Not checked against an independent source.
age_at_collection_daysinteger days postnatalAge at sample collection in days. Archive ages without a unit are read as years for projects outside the in-depth curation; ranges and timepoint lists are never values. The age category (neonate … elderly) is derived from this value, else from the sample's or project's stated life stage (basis column).
sexfemale, male, unknownArchive attribute, table column or a single-sex cohort statement in the paper text. Not checked against an independent source.
detailed_locationderived: location_site, location_locality, location_regionThe most specific place below the country the source gives — 'site, locality, region' with empty parts dropped. It is not a value of its own: its parts are location_site, location_locality and location_region, each with its own evidence row.
lifestylehunter_gatherer, pastoralist, traditional_agriculturalist, rural_non_industrialized, urban_industrialized, transitional_or_migrant, isolated_religious_community, indigenous_community, nomadic, vegetarian_or_vegan, athlete, military, spaceflight_or_analog, institutionalized, extreme_environment, other_lifestyle, unknownControlled vocabulary; committed only when the source states it — never inferred from the country.
collection_dateAs specific as the source gives it (YYYY, YYYY-MM, YYYY-MM-DD or an ISO interval for a stated sampling period); placeholders (missing / not collected / restricted) stay unknown.
interventionwhat this sample's person had received IN THE PROJECT at or before sampling (';'-joined sorted codes; placebo / no_intervention for control arms and pre-intervention baselines). Per-sample from archive attributes; the study-level list of administered interventions is catalog_studies.interventions (from the abstract) and is not copied onto samples — controls of an FMT trial are not FMT samples.
antibiotic_exposureyes, no, unknown"yes"/"no". "no" from a source that documents no exposure (attribute, table, or an exclusion window ≥ 1 month in the Methods), never from "no mention".
bmiBody-mass index from archive attributes or tables; plausibility window 10–80; never derived from a cohort mean.
timepoint_labelstudy's own label (free text)Compact tokens (D6, M4, baseline) are labels, not ages, unless the project confirms the unit.
comparison_groupThe group of the project's own comparison, in the paper's wording; assigned from a sample attribute or name stem only where two independent readings of the paper agreed on every value's group.
diethabitual diet pattern stated by the source
smoking_statusnever, former, currentSmoking status of the sampled person, as the source states it.
medication';'-joined sorted codes; antibiotics are antibiotic_exposure, not here
stool_consistency_bristolBristol stool form scale 1–7 as stated; textual forms (hard / normal / loose / watery) are mapped 2 / 4 / 6 / 7 with confidence 0.7
collection_yearleading YYYY of collection_date (start of an interval)
health_condition_detailFree text behind the code (diagnosis, group label, criterion).
location_regionFirst-level administrative region below the country (state, province, prefecture …); English exonym, no country name.
latitudeDecimal degrees from lat_lon / geographic-location attributes of the archive record or a supplementary table, rounded to 4 decimals.
longitudeDecimal degrees; same source row as latitude.
health_condition_nameName of the condition this sample's health-condition label names, in the words of its ontology term. Empty where the label names no condition. A name can be present where no ontology term was found, and then both identifier columns are empty. Not a value of its own: it is the label mapped to a term, so it carries no source beside it.
location_localityCity, town, village or district where people were recruited — not where the laboratory is.
health_condition_efo_idIdentifier of that condition in EFO. EFO takes many disease terms from MONDO, HP and Orphanet and keeps their identifiers, so most values start with MONDO: and some with HP: or Orphanet:. This is the identifier of the condition: two conditions never share it.
health_condition_mondo_idMONDO term of that condition. Empty where EFO links no MONDO term, and where the nearest MONDO term is only a broader one; it is not an identifier of the condition, and two conditions may carry the same one.
location_siteNamed recruitment site (hospital, clinic, school, cohort field station, community name) in the source's own words.
family_idthe source's own family / household / dyad / twin-pair identifier; samples of related people share it (within the project); never a cohort or study id
family_rolemother, father, parent, child, twin, sibling, partner, grandparent, other_relative, household_memberRole of the sampled person in the family, dyad, household or twin pair the source names.
intervention_detailFree text behind the intervention code, in the source's own words.
lifestyle_detailNamed population or the source's own words behind the lifestyle code (Hadza, Hutterite colony, elite rugby players …).
shared_setting_typehousehold, village / community, tribe, daycare / school, care home, ward, military unit, team, workplaceA place or group below the project that at least two sampled people share — never the whole cohort or the city of recruitment.
shared_setting_idthe source's name / code of that unit (e.g. 'H12', 'Mbomo', 'daycare D3')
diet_detailFree text behind the diet code, in the source's own words.
medication_detailFree text behind the medication code, in the source's own words.
preterm_statuspreterm | term | unknownInfant field: term requires ≥ 37 weeks when derived from a criterion.
delivery_modevaginal | c_section | c_section_elective | c_section_emergency | unknownInfant field: vaginal / caesarean; categories as stated by the source.
gestational_age_weeksfloat weeks at birthInfant field: completed weeks at birth.
feeding_modeexclusive_breast | mixed | formula | weaned | unknownInfant field: breast / mixed / formula as stated; boolean feeding columns never become a value.
birth_weight_gramsinteger gramsInfant field; no external truth set.
probiotic_exposureyes | no | unknownInfant field: any probiotic given to the infant.
maternal_antibioticsyes | no | unknown (pregnancy, labour or lactation)Infant field: intrapartum or pregnancy antibiotics to the mother.
nec_statusyes | no | unknown (necrotising enterocolitis diagnosis)Infant field: necrotising enterocolitis diagnosis.
hmo_supplementationyes | no | unknownInfant field: HMO-supplemented formula.

Confidence

Confidence is a tier set by the engine that produced the value. It orders values by reliability; it is not a probability. We checked it in a blind audit of release R2026.41 (data package 1.34.0): 2,400 values were drawn at random within field, source type and confidence tier, and an independent reader derived each value again from the cited source without seeing ours. Disagreements were read again, the last time with both values in view. 2,204 values could be judged.

Shares are weighted to the catalog, with 95 % intervals. “Not confirmed from the cited source alone” means the reader, given only the cited text, would not have recorded the value. It covers cohort-wide statements applied to every sample of a project, time points and groups decoded from sample names, regions worked out from coordinates, and subject identifiers generated where a project has one sample per person. Such a value is not shown to be false, but it rests on more than the quote beside it. The tiers from 0.70 upward are in order; the two lowest are not: the 0.55–0.65 tier holds mostly regions worked out from coordinates and generated subject identifiers, and a smaller share of it was confirmed (30.6 %) than of the tier at 0.50 and below (62.4 %).

Package data dictionary

Every column of every package table is documented in the data dictionary, including the columns that record where each value came from. The catalog tables are catalog_studies, catalog_sample_metadata_wide, catalog_sample_determinations and catalog_runs; the registry tables are registry_*.
