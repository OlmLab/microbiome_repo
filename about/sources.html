Sources & acknowledgements

About › Sources & acknowledgements

Source list reviewed 2026-11-12.

Everything on this site is derived from public archives and open literature; this page names those sources and their terms, the software and models the pipeline uses, the funding, and the other projects whose curation is incorporated here.

Development of Microbiome Repo is supported by a grant from Anthropic to the Olm Lab (University of Colorado Boulder) for organising human microbiome metadata.

Data sources

sourcewhat we take from itterms
European Nucleotide Archive (ENA) / INSDC (SRA, DDBJ)Study, sample, run and BioSample records and attributes for every screened study (portal API); these are the archive records values are read from.INSDC: free and unrestricted access to all records.
NCBI BioProject and BioSampleBioProject records (titles, descriptions, publications, organisations) and BioSample attributes; E-utilities.NCBI public-domain data policy.
Europe PMCPaper ↔ accession links, abstracts, open-access full text (JATS) and supplementary files — the supplementary table, paper text and abstract a value can come from; author lists.Europe PMC open-access subset; individual article licences apply to reuse of text.
Sandpiper (SingleM community profiles of public metagenomes)Per-run community profiles for every catalog project that Sandpiper has profiled, and the count of profiled runs per project.CC-BY (Woodcroft et al. 2025).
UBERONAnchors of the body-site vocabulary.CC-BY 3.0.
GTDBTaxonomy of the Sandpiper community panels (release stated on each panel).CC-BY-SA 4.0.

Software and models

componentrolelicence
DuckDB-WASMIn-browser SQL over the parquet tables (sample and registry explorers); pinned 1.29.0 from jsDelivr.MIT.
D3, TopoJSON and Natural Earth country shapesThe globe on the home page and the taxon maps (D3 7.9, topojson-client 3.1, Natural Earth 1:110m via world-atlas). Boundaries are shown only to display sample counts and imply no position on the status of any territory; Crimea is drawn as part of Ukraine (UN General Assembly resolution 68/262), which differs from the Natural Earth default.ISC / BSD-3-Clause (D3, TopoJSON); Natural Earth is public domain.
DataTables and jQuerySortable project, cohort and scope tables.MIT.
Apache Parquet / Arrow, pandas, DuckDB, Jinja2, Python-MarkdownPipeline and site generator (microbiome_repo-pipeline).Apache-2.0 / BSD / MIT (see the repository).
Anthropic Claude modelsModel-assisted classification and extraction of values from archive records and papers, always with a verbatim quote; the role and the model id are recorded on every model-derived value.Anthropic usage policies.

We gratefully acknowledge the teams behind African Human Microbiome Portal (H3ABioNet), BugSigDB, curatedMetagenomicData 3 (cMD 3), GMrepo v3, HMP / iHMP data portal, HumanMetagenomeDB, mBodyMap, Meta2DB (LLNL), MicrobeAtlas, MicrobiomeDB (VEuPathDB): their curation is incorporated here as external-resource values, each one named as coming from that resource and linking back to its record there, and users of those values should cite the reference listed for the resource alongside this catalog. Resources that publish no licence are redistributed with attribution only; if you maintain one of them and prefer different terms, open an issue and we will comply.

Citing and licence

How to cite this release: About › How to cite.

Curated tables (catalog_* and registry_*) are released under CC-BY-4.0; archive metadata remains under INSDC terms; Sandpiper community profiles under CC-BY. When you use values from this catalog, please also cite the primary studies — every project page lists its papers.
