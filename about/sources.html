Sources and acknowledgements

About › Sources & acknowledgements

Everything on this site is derived from public archives and open literature; this page names those sources and their terms, the software and models the pipeline uses, the funding, and the other projects that curate human-microbiome metadata — with an honest assessment of what each could add to the catalog and how it would join. Last reviewed 2026-09-28.

Development of Microbiome Repo is supported by a grant from Anthropic to the Olm Lab (University of Colorado Boulder) for organising human microbiome metadata.

External curated resources incorporated

Other groups have curated human microbiome metadata before us. Where their per-sample or per-study annotations join our samples through archive accessions, we incorporate them as route R2 evidence rows — evidence_source = external.<resource>, the locator a link to the record at the source, the quote the source's raw value — after the study's own supplementary tables in precedence (a sample's own archive attribute always wins). We are grateful to these projects; please cite them when you use values that carry their evidence source.

Acknowledgement. We gratefully acknowledge the teams behind African Human Microbiome Portal (H3ABioNet), BugSigDB, curatedMetagenomicData 3 (cMD 3), GMrepo v3, HMP / iHMP data portal, HumanMetagenomeDB, mBodyMap, Meta2DB (LLNL), MicrobeAtlas, MicrobiomeDB (VEuPathDB): their curation work is incorporated here as route-R2 evidence, every incorporated value links back to its source record (evidence_source = external.<resource>), and users of those values should cite the reference listed for the resource alongside this catalog. Resources that publish no licence are redistributed with attribution only; if you maintain one of them and prefer different terms, open an issue and we will comply.
