About

Microbiome Repo was created in the Olm Lab at the University of Colorado Boulder and is funded by Anthropic's AI for Science program.

About this page

Two tiers. The registry lists every shotgun-metagenome study screened in ENA, NCBI SRA and DDBJ, classified for human host, body site, life stage, assay and access (7,385 human studies of 64,369). The catalog is the curated part: human-associated shotgun DNA and RNA projects from every body site and every age (5,086 projects, 813,356 samples). Every catalog value carries the quote it was read from, with its source and a confidence.
