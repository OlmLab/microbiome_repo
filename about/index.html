About

Microbiome Repo was created in the Olm Lab at the University of Colorado Boulder and is funded by Anthropic's AI for Science program.

About this page

It has two tiers: a registry of every shotgun-metagenome study reached in ENA — INSDC BioProjects deposited in ENA, NCBI SRA or DDBJ — classified for human host, body site, life stage, assay and access (6,893 human studies of 64,369), and a curated catalog of the human-gut studies among them, all ages (2,865 studies, 571,712 samples), with per-sample metadata in which every value carries a verbatim evidence quote, a route and a confidence.

Scope
How the 2,865 catalog studies are cut from the registry: the study rule, the funnel, and the gut studies left out and why. Methods
Registry enumeration and classification, the four curation routes (R1–R4) and their precedence, evidence and confidence rules, cohorts, releases, limitations. Sources & acknowledgements
Archives, literature, software and models behind the catalog; the external curated resources incorporated as R2 evidence; funding. Fields documentation
All 37 fields grouped core / key / infant extension with vocabulary, coverage, route mix and confidence; the package data dictionary. Downloads & releases
The data package, per-table downloads, the release history and what changed in each release. Contribute
Studies whose core fields are still missing, and how a per-sample table or a paper pointer unlocks them.
