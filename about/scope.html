Scope: from the registry to the catalog

About › Scope

The registry lists every shotgun-metagenome INSDC BioProject reached through ENA and classifies its host; the catalog curates the human studies whose runs include the human gut / stool, at every age.

About this page

Study rule (config/packs/gut.yaml): registry_studies.host_human in {yes, mixed} and assay in {shotgun_dna, mixed} and gut_stool in body_sites, OR in_infant_catalog == include. Sample rule: registry_biosamples of those studies (all body sites kept; body_site_class marks non-gut samples) + the curated infant samples. Every sample of a catalog study is kept, with a body_site_class (primary = gut/stool, unknown = no site attribute, excluded/linked = another site of a gut study) so that nothing is silently dropped.

Age categories

Age at collection in days → category (config/packs/gut.yaml): neonate 0–28 d · infant 29–1100 d · child 1101–4382 d · adolescent 4383–6574 d · adult 6575–23741 d · elderly 23742–∞ d. Without a numeric age the category falls back to the sample's stated life stage, then to a cohort-wide statement (R3/R4), then to the study's primary life stage; the basis is recorded per sample.

Gut studies not in the catalog (1,501)

Registry studies with gut / stool among their body sites that fail the study rule — host not human (yes or mixed), assay not shotgun (amplicon, genome, transcriptome), or classification still pending. They stay in the registry with their evidence and enter the catalog when a later cycle resolves them.
