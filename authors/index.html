About this page

An author is credited with a project only through a paper that generated that project's data (Europe PMC author list of the paper; the paper is the data's publication according to the BioProject record, the paper's data-availability statement, or its full text). Papers that re-analyse or cite public data credit no one. Links where it is unclear whether the paper produced or re-used the data are listed separately as possibly associated. Name variants that share an ORCID (e.g. "Morgan X" and "Morgan XC") are merged. Each project appears as a preview card: accession, title, samples, country / top condition, year.

Caveats: names are matched from Europe PMC author strings — variants without a shared ORCID stay separate (e.g. "Aagaard K" and "Aagaard KM"); projects whose data paper was not found have no authors; consortium names are kept as group authors. Wrong or missing projects under your name? Tell us and we will correct it in the next update.
