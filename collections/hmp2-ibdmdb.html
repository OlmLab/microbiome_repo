HMP2 / IBDMDB longitudinal IBD multi-omics cohort

Benchmark and reference cohorts · collection id hmp2-ibdmdb

The Integrative HMP inflammatory bowel disease cohort (IBDMDB): 132 subjects with Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis or no IBD, followed for one year with stool metagenomes, metatranscriptomes and biopsies at five US centres.
