Faecal microbiota transplantation trials

Study design · collection id fmt-trials

Randomised and single-arm FMT trials with shotgun follow-up across indications — ulcerative colitis, IBS, obesity, Parkinson's disease, hypertension, cirrhosis, diabetic neuropathy, immunotherapy resistance in melanoma and lung cancer, and CPE decolonisation — plus FMT donor series.
