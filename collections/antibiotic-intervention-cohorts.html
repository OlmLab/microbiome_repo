Antibiotic intervention and recovery cohorts

Study design · collection id antibiotic-intervention-cohorts

Studies in which antibiotics were administered by design or documented as the exposure of interest, with before/after shotgun sampling: healthy-volunteer courses (cefprozil, ciprofloxacin, beta-lactams), probiotic-after-antibiotic trials, travellers'-diarrhoea regimens, mass azithromycin distribution and preterm-infant exposure.
