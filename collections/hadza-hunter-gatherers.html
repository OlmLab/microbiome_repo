Hadza hunter-gatherers (Tanzania)

Population cohorts · collection id hadza-hunter-gatherers

All shotgun deposits from the Hadza of Tanzania, the reference hunter-gatherer population: Rampelli 2015, the seasonal-cycling cohort (Smits 2017) and the deep-sequenced adults and infants of Carter 2023 / Olm 2022 (with Nepali comparison groups in the same BioProject).
