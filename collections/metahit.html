MetaHIT European gut metagenome cohorts

Benchmark and reference cohorts · collection id metahit

The MetaHIT consortium deposits underlying the first gut gene catalogue and the metagenomic-species work: Danish and Spanish adults (Qin 2010), Danish obese/lean adults (Le Chatelier 2013, Nielsen 2014) and the integrated gene catalogue samples (Li 2014).
