Centenarians and extreme longevity

Age bands · collection id centenarians-extreme-longevity

Cohorts recruited for exceptional age: Japanese centenarians (Sato 2021), Sardinian semi-supercentenarians (Rampelli 2020), Chinese longevity areas (Guangxi and Sichuan cohorts), longevous families and the Greek Blue Zone cohort.
