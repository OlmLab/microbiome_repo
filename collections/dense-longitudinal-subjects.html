Densely sampled subjects (≥ 5 gut samples per subject)

Study design · collection id dense-longitudinal-subjects

Every subject in the catalog with at least five gut samples linked to the same subject_id — the time-series slice for strain tracking, stability and perturbation-recovery analyses. Dominated by infant birth cohorts (TEDDY, DIABIMMUNE), IBDMDB and adult time-series studies.
