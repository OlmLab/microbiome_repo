HMP1 healthy adult reference (Human Microbiome Project, WGS)

Benchmark and reference cohorts · collection id hmp1-healthy-adults

The NIH Human Microbiome Project phase 1 shotgun metagenomes of clinically healthy US adults (Houston and St Louis), the most reused healthy-adult reference set; the BioProject spans all HMP body sites, so restrict to gut samples with body_site_class.
